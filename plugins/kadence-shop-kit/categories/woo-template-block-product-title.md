## Woo Template Block – Product: Title

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-title/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-title/)

When creating Woo Templates, you can access the Product: Title block. The block displays the title of the product which your template will display.

Find the Product: Title block under the Woo Template Blocks section and add it to your template like any other block.

![Product Title Block](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Product-Title.jpeg)Product Title Block
## Product: Title Block Settings

The Product: Title block displays the title of the product. You can style the title using the block’s settings. There are general settings, styling settings, and spacing settings.

![Product Title Block Settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Product-Title-Block-Settings-1024x506.jpeg)Product Title Block SettingsGeneral Settings allow you to align the text for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Styling settings adjust text color and typography settings like font size, line height, and letter spacing. There are also settings to transform the text, font family, and font weight.

Lastly, the spacing settings add margin and padding to the text, which you may set for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
