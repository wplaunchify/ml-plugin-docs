## Woo Template Block – Product: Rating

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-rating/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-rating/)

The Product: Rating block allows you to display the rating of a product on your Woo Template.

![Product Rating Example](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/productrating_display.png)

How To Enable Product Reviews

In order to see a product rating, you must enable product reviews in your WooCommerce settings.

Go to your WordPress Dashboard, navigate to WooCommerce > Settings, and then click on the Products tab.

Look for the Product Reviews option and enable the checkbox selection.

![Enable WooCommerce Reviews](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/woocommerce_reviews.png)

Product: Rating Block

You can add a new Product: Rating block to your Woo Template just like any other block. You can find it under the Woo Templates block section.

![Product: Rating block](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_rating_wt-493x1024-1.png)

Product: Rating Block Settings

You can customize your Product: Rating block by accessing its Block Settings.

![Product: Rating Block Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/productrating_blocksettings-scaled.png)

**General Settings –** You can adjust the alignment of your Product: Rating block for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

![Product: Rating general block setting](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_rating_general-295x300-1.png)

---

**Rating Style –**You can customize the colors of your Product: Rating block and typography settings for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

![Product: Rating style settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_rating_style.png)

---

**Spacing Settings –**You can adjust the padding and margin of your Product: Rating block for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

![Spacing Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/spacing_settings.jpg)

---

**Advanced Settings –**You can add custom CSS classes to your Product: Rating block.

![Advanced Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_gallery_advanced.jpg)
