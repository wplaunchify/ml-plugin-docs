## Woo Template Block – Product: Image

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/troubleshooting-kadence-shop-kit-product-images-not-showing/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/troubleshooting-kadence-shop-kit-product-images-not-showing/)

When creating Woo Templates, you can access the Product: Image block. This block displays the featured image of the current product.

Find the Product: Image block under the Woo Template Blocks section and add it to your template like any other block.

![Select Product Image Block](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_image_block.png)

Product: Image Block Settings

![Product Image Block Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_image_blocksettings-1024x472-1.png)

**General Settings**

You can enable the option to force your image to fill the container and change the alignment settings for your Product: Image block.

![Product Image General Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_image_general.jpg)

**Spacing Settings**

You can adjust the padding and margin of your Product: Image block.

![Spacing Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_gallery_spacing.png)

**Advanced Settings**

You can add custom CSS classes to your Product: Image block.

![Advanced Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_gallery_advanced.jpg)
