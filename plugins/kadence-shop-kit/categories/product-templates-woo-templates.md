## Product Templates (Woo Templates)

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/product-templates-woo-templates/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/product-templates-woo-templates/)

Kadence **Shop Kit** features the **Product** **Templates** **(Woo Templates)** feature. Using Woo Templates, you can control WooCommerce Product Loop Items, Single Product Pages, and Product Archive pages. Kadence Product Templates feature various Woo Template blocks, such as the Product: Gallery and the Product: Add to Cart blocks, allowing you to customize various **WooCommerce** pages dynamically.

## Enabling Woo Templates

In order to create a **Woo** **Template**, you must install, activate, and license the **Kadence** **Shop** **Kit** plugin. ([Click here to learn more](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/shop-kit/install-activate-kadence-shop-kit/).) Once the *Shop Kit plugin* is activated on the website, navigate to the **Dashboard -> Shop Kit** page and go to the **Product** **Templates** tab.

![Enable Product Templates](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1024,h_836,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1780587509/prod/Enable-Product-Templates-1/Enable-Product-Templates-1.jpg?_i=AA)*Enable Product Templates* and save the changes. Afterward, refresh the page. Then, navigate to the **Dashboard -> Products -> Woo Templates** page. At the top of the *Woo Templates page*, click on the **Add New Woo Templates** button to begin adding a new *Woo Template*.

![Add New Woo Template](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1024,h_850,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1780587612/prod/Add-New-Woo-Template-1/Add-New-Woo-Template-1.jpg?_i=AA)This will open a new **Woo** **Template** **Post** with the option to select the template type. There are a total of four *Woo Template Types*. Learn more about each template type below.

![Shop Kit - Template Types](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1014,h_708,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1780587727/prod/Template-Types/Template-Types.jpg?_i=AA)
## Types of Woo Templates

The available Woo Template types are: **Product** **Catalog** **Loop** **Item** templates, **Single** **Product** templates, **Product** **Archive** templates, and **Quickview** templates. Use core blocks, Kadence blocks, and **Woo Template blocks** to design and style your *Woo Templates*. Learn about each template type below.

Tip[Click here](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/shop-kit/enable-woo-template-blocks/) for a list of all of the available Woo Template blocks.
### Single Product Templates

**Single** **Product Woo** **Templates** give you full control over how your individual product pages appear.

You can use popular *Woo Template blocks*, such as the [Product Gallery](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/shop-kit/product-gallery-block/), [Product Price](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/shop-kit/product-price-block/), and [Product Add to Cart](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/shop-kit/product-add-to-cart-block/) blocks, to build the perfect product page layout.

Single Product Woo Templates offer three predesigned layouts to help you get started.

### Product Archive Templates

**Product Archive Woo Templates** let you customize the layout of your *WooCommerce Archive pages*, including your main Shop page and product category or tag pages. 

Choose between the **Advanced Query Loop**block or the **Archive Main Query Woo Template**block to display and style your products as preferred.

### Catalog Loop Item Templates

**Catalog Loop Item Woo Templates** can be used to take control over product loops across your website. *Catalog Loop Item templates* can be used anywhere the default product loop is, allowing you to control loop items universally as preferred.

*Catalog Loop Items*can also be used as a [Query Loop Card](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/blocks/advanced/queries/advanced-query-loop-block/#how-adv-query-loops-and-cards-work) on an Advanced Query, allowing you to reuse the design in custom queries.

Kadence Shop Kit features the [Product Quickview](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/kadence/shop-kit/product-quickview-button-block/)feature. This feature allows users to view detailed product information and add items to their cart without leaving the current page.

## Woo Template Settings

Use the **Woo** **Template** **Settings** to control where the current Woo Template will override and who will see it. Click on the **pencil/paper icon** in the top right of the editor.

Each template type will have available Woo Template Settings to control where and to whom the template will appear. This also includes the option to set an expiration on the Woo Template.
