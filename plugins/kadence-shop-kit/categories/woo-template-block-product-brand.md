## Woo Template Block – Product: Brand

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-brand/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-brand/)

Kadence Shop Kit allows you to add brands to your products. The Product: Brand block lets you display the Product’s Brand on a Woo Template.

![Product Brand](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Product-Brand.png)
## Enabling Product Brands

You can enable Product Brands in Shop Kit. The WordPress Dashboard Shop Kit page has a section labeled Product Brands. You can Enable Product Brand Options here.

![Enable Product Brands](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Enable-Product-Brands.png)Once you have enabled product brands, you can add a new brand in your Products > Brands.![New Product Brand](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/New-Product-Brand.png)When editing a product, you can add a brand to your product by finding the brands box and selecting a brand.![Product Brand Taxonomy](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Product-Brand-Taxonomy.png)You can learn more about configuring product brands by [Clicking Here](https://www.liquidweb.com/docs/kadence-shop-kit/wooextras-product-brand-configuration/).

## Product: Brand

You can add a new Product: Brand block to your Woo Template by finding it under the Woo Templates section.

![Product Brand Block](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Product-Brand-Block-481x1024.png)
## Product: Brand Block Settings

You can customize your Product Brand by accessing the Block Settings.

![Product Brand Block Settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Product-Brand-Block-Settings.png)**Spacing Settings**
The Spacing Settings allow you to adjust the padding and margin on your Product: Brand block for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.![Product Brand Spacing](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_546,h_500,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1780610659/prod/Product-Brand-Spacing_45248503243/Product-Brand-Spacing_45248503243.png?_i=AA)**Advanced Settings**
