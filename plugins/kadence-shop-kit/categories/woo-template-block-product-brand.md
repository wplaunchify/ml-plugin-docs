## Woo Template Block – Product: Brand

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-brand/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-brand/)

Kadence Shop Kit allows you to add brands to your products. The Product: Brand block lets you display the Product’s Brand on a Woo Template.

![Product Brand](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Brand.png)

Enabling Product Brands

You can enable Product Brands in Shop Kit. The WordPress Dashboard Shop Kit page has a section labeled Product Brands. You can Enable Product Brand Options here.

![Enable Product Brands](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Enable-Product-Brands-scaled.png)

Once you have enabled product brands, you can add a new brand in your Products > Brands.

![New Product Brand](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/New-Product-Brand.png)

When editing a product, you can add a brand to your product by finding the brands box and selecting a brand.

![Product Brand Taxonomy](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Brand-Taxonomy.png)

You can learn more about configuring product brands by [Clicking Here](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/product-brands/).

Product: Brand

You can add a new Product: Brand block to your Woo Template by finding it under the Woo Templates section.

![Product Brand Block](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Brand-Block-481x1024-1.png)

Product: Brand Block Settings

You can customize your Product Brand by accessing the Block Settings.

![Product Brand Block Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Brand-Block-Settings-scaled.png)

**Spacing Settings**
The Spacing Settings allow you to adjust the padding and margin on your Product: Brand block for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

![Product Brand Spacing](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Brand-Spacing.png)

**Advanced Settings**
The Advanced Settings allow you to add Custom CSS Class(es) to your Product: Brand block.

![Product Brand Advanced](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Brand-Advanced.png)
