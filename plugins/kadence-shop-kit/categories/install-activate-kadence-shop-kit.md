## How to Install & Activate Kadence Shop Kit

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/install-activate-kadence-shop-kit/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/install-activate-kadence-shop-kit/)

**Kadence Shop Kit** extends WooCommerce functionality by adding advanced features such as WooTemplates (single product & archive builders), variation swatches, custom checkout fields, product gallery sliders, and quick-view modals.

The Kadence Shop Kit plugin is available in the [Kadence Pro and Elite Plans](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/shop-kit/). It is not sold as an individual plugin.
