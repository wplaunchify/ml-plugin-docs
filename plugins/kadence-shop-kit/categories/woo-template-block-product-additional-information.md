## Woo Template Block Product: Additional Information

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-additional-information/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-additional-information/)

The Product: Additional Information block allows you to display the attributes of a product.

![Product: Additional Information](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Product-Additional-Showcase-e1670945105589.png)
## Adding Additional Information

The Product: Additional Information block displays the current attributes of a product. You can add attributes to your product. First, you will need to edit the product. Then look for the product data box. In the product data box, there will be a tab labeled attributes. Here you can add attributes that will display on your Product: Additional Information block.

![Add attributes to product](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/product-additional-information-product-setting.gif)
### Product: Additional Information Block

You can add a new Product: Additional Information block to your Woo Template just like any other Block. You can find it under the Woo Templates block section.

![Add Additional Information Block](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/12/Product-Additional-Block-e1670945779749-440x1024.png)
### Product: Additional Information Block

You can customize your Additional Information block by accessing its Block Settings.
