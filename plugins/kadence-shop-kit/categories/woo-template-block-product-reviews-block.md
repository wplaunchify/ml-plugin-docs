## Woo Template Block – Product: Reviews Block

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-reviews-block/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-reviews-block/)

The Product Reviews Block allows users to leave reviews on your product.

![Leaving a review](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/leavingareview.gif)

The Product: Reviews block allows you to display the reviews of a product on your Woo Template. You can add the Product: Reviews block just like any other block.

![Product reviews block selection.](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_reviews_blockselection-487x1024-1.png)

Product Reviews:

You can enable Product Reviews by going to WooCommerce > Settings > Products and enabling Product Reviews.

![Enable Product Reviews](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/enable_product_reviews-1024x588-1.png)

Product: Reviews Block Settings

You can add the Product: Reviews block to your Woo Template like any other block. Then you can customize your Product: Reviews Block Settings.

![Product Reviews Block Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_reviews_block_Settings-1024x420-1.png)

**General Settings-** Here you can customize the Product Reviews Layout. You can choose your layout from the inherit, single-column, or two-column options. You can use the responsive controls to select a different layout for different devices.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_reviews_general_settings.png)

**Spacing Settings –**You can adjust the padding and margin of your Product: Reviews block for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

![Spacing Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/spacing_settings.jpg)

**Advanced Settings –**You can add custom CSS classes to your Product: Reviews block.

![Advanced Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_gallery_advanced.jpg)
