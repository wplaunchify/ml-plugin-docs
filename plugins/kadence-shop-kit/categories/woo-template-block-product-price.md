## Woo Template Block – Product: Price

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-price/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-price/)

Ideally, your product page shows the price of the product. Therefore, Kadence Shop Kit provides the Product: Price block to display the price of a product in your template. Using the price block, you can place the product’s price wherever you want in your design.

![Product Price](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Price.jpeg)Product Price

Product: Price Block Settings

General settings, price style, and spacing settings are available for the Product: Price block.

![Product Price Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Product-Price-Settings-1024x517-1.jpeg)Product Price Settings

General Settings allow you to align the text for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Price style settings adjust text color and typography settings like font size, line height, and letter spacing. There are also settings to transform the text, font family, and font weight.

Lastly, the spacing settings add margin and padding to the text, which you may set for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
