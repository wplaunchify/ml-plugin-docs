## Woo Template Block – Product: Related

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-related/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-related/)

The Product: Related block allows you to display related products on your Woo Template.

![Product: Related](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_related_demo-1024x713-1.png)

Product: Related Block

You can add a new Product: Related block to your Woo Template just like any other block. You can find it under the Woo Templates block section.

![Product Rating Block](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_related_block-587x1024-1.png)

Product Related: Block Settings

You can customize your Product: Related block by accessing its Block Settings.

![Product: Related block settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_related_settings-scaled.png)

**General Settings –** You can enable or disable the related products heading on your Product: Related block. You can also adjust the number of columns used on your Product: Related block. The related items limit setting allows you to limit the number of related items shown.

![Product: Related general settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_related_general.png)

---

**Product Styling –** You can customize the product title color and the product background color. You can also adjust the product title typography settings for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

![Product: Related styling settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_related_styling-360x1024-1.png)

---

**Spacing Settings –**You can adjust the padding and margin of your Product: Related block for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

![Spacing Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/spacing_settings.jpg)

---

**Advanced Settings –**You can add custom CSS classes to your Product: Related block.

![Advanced Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/product_gallery_advanced.jpg)
