## Woo Template Block – Product: Related

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-related/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-shop-kit/woo-template-block-product-related/)

The Product: Related block allows you to display related products on your Woo Template.

![Product: Related](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/product_related_demo-1024x713.png)
## Product: Related Block

You can add a new Product: Related block to your Woo Template just like any other block. You can find it under the Woo Templates block section.

![Product Rating Block](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/product_related_block-587x1024.png)
### Product Related: Block Settings

You can customize your Product: Related block by accessing its Block Settings.

![Product: Related block settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/product_related_settings.png)**General Settings –** You can enable or disable the related products heading on your Product: Related block. You can also adjust the number of columns used on your Product: Related block. The related items limit setting allows you to limit the number of related items shown.![Product: Related general settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/product_related_general.png)---**Product Styling –** You can customize the product title color and the product background color. You can also adjust the product title typography settings for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.![Product: Related styling settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/product_related_styling-360x1024.png)---**Spacing Settings –**You can adjust the padding and margin of your Product: Related block for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.![Spacing Settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/spacing_settings.jpg)---**Advanced Settings –**You can add custom CSS classes to your Product: Related block.![Advanced Settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/product_gallery_advanced.jpg)Was this article helpful?Yes NoThank you for your input.Thank you for your feedback.
