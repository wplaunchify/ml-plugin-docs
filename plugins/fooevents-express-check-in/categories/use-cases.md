## Key FooEvents features for conferences

Explore the conference-friendly features that help you sell tickets, manage registrations, communicate with attendees, run check-ins, support virtual sessions, and keep your event data under your control.

### Conference ticket sales

Sell conference tickets directly through WooCommerce using your existing cart, checkout, payment gateways, taxes, coupons, customer accounts, and order workflows.

### No commission or ticket fees

Keep more of your revenue by selling tickets from your own website without paying per-ticket commissions to a third-party ticketing platform.

### Multiple ticket types

Create ticket options such as General Admission, VIP, Early Bird, Student, Member, Speaker, Sponsor, Exhibitor, or Virtual Access using WooCommerce variations.

### Capacity and stock control

Manage conference capacity using WooCommerce stock settings so you can control ticket availability and reduce the risk of overselling.

### Attendee detail capture

Use the [FooEvents Custom Attendee Fields](https://www.fooevents.com/products/fooevents-custom-attendee-fields/) extension to collect custom attendee information your conference needs, such as names, company details, dietary requirements, accessibility requests, session choices, or consent confirmations.

### Branded ticket emails

Send [professional HTML ticket emails](https://www.fooevents.com/products/ticket-themes/) that include conference details, attendee information, QR codes, barcodes, calendar links, and your event branding.

### PDF tickets

Use the [FooEvents PDF Tickets](https://www.fooevents.com/products/fooevents-pdf-tickets/) extension to attach downloadable PDF tickets and booking confirmations to ticket emails so attendees can save, print, or present their tickets when they arrive.

### Add to Calendar support

Include calendar links and ICS attachments in ticket emails so attendees can add conference dates and reminders to their calendar apps.

### Multi-day schedule support

Use the [FooEvents Multi-day](https://www.fooevents.com/products/fooevents-multi-day/) extensionto manage conferences that run across multiple days and display relevant date information on tickets, emails, reports, and attendee records.

### Workshop and session bookings

Let attendees book specific sessions, workshops, training slots, or breakout activities using [FooEvents Bookings](https://www.fooevents.com/products/fooevents-bookings/).

### Zoom meetings and webinars

Create or assign Zoom meetings and webinars and automatically register attendees when they purchase virtual conference tickets.

### Hybrid event support

Sell in-person and virtual attendance options for the same conference while keeping tickets, orders, and attendee data connected in WooCommerce.

### Reserved seating

[Use FooEvents Seating](https://www.fooevents.com/products/fooevents-seating/) when your conference includes assigned seating for talks, awards evenings, gala dinners, or theatre-style venues.

### Mobile check-ins

Use the free [FooEvents Check-ins apps](https://www.fooevents.com/products/apps/) for iOS and Android to scan QR codes or barcodes, validate tickets, search attendees, and manage entry.

### Offline check-ins

Keep checking attendees in when venue internet is unreliable, then sync check-in data once the device reconnects.

### Door sales and on-site registration

Use [FooEvents POS](https://www.fooevents.com/products/fooevents-pos/) or [FooSales](https://www.foosales.com/) to sell tickets in person at the registration desk, box office, or venue entrance.

### Badges and stationery

Design and print name tags, badges, wristbands, labels, and physical tickets using the FooEvents Stationery Builder.

### Attendee exports

Export attendee and ticket data to CSV for planning, registration desks, sponsor lists, reporting, communication, or use with other tools.

### Event reports and insights

View ticket sales, revenue, tickets sold, available tickets, check-ins, check-outs, and performance reports from inside WordPress.

### Conference calendar display

Use FooEvents Calendar to display conference dates, sessions, workshops, or related events in a calendar or list view.

### Email and automation workflows

Connect FooEvents with tools such as Mailchimp and AutomateWoo to support attendee communication, reminders, follow-ups, and event automation.

### Private data ownership

Keep customer, order, attendee, and ticket data in your own WordPress and WooCommerce database instead of handing it to a hosted ticketing platform.
