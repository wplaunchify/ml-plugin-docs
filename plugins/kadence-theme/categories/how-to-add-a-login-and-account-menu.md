## How to add a login and account Menu

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-add-a-login-and-account-menu/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-add-a-login-and-account-menu/)

Login and Account menu options are a part of [Kadence Theme Kit Pro](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/).

To add these to your Kadence site’s header, enable **Header Addons** in your Kadence dashboard at Appearance > Kadence.

![Enabling Header Addons in Kadence Pro](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/header-addons-kadence-pro.png)To add Login and Account items to your site’s header head over to the WordPress Customizer at **Appearance > Customize**.

Click on the **Header** section.

Locate **Account** under Available Items and drag it to your desired position in the header builder.

![Dragging Account item in the Header Builder](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/adding-account-item-header-builder.jpg)To edit the settings for Account item, either click on the pencil icon next to it or the gear icon in the header builder.

![Settings for Account header item](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/account-header-builder-settings.jpg)That takes you to the Header Account where you can find GENERAL and DESIGN settings for the Account item.

![Header Account settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/header-account-settings-kadence.png)The default “Account” label text can be changed by entering a custom one in the **Account Label** field.

If you want the Header Account item in the header to link to a specific URL, that can be entered in the **Label/Icon/Image URL** field.

To make a navigation menu appear for your site’s logged-in users when you click on the Account element in the header, click **Select Menu** and pick/create a menu of your choice under the **Logged In Account** location.

To configure the settings for logged out users, click on LOGGED OUT VIEW.

![Header Account Logged Out View](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/header-account-logged-out-view.png)To configure the appearance of Header Account item go to DESIGN tab.
