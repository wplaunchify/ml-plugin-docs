## Importing and Exporting Kadence Elements

**Kadence Hooked Elements** can be used in many different cases, from adding content to theme hooks across your website, to completely replacing single-post-content with custom templates. There may be an instance where you would like to export your Kadence Elements and import them onto a different website. This document will go over the **Kadence Elements Import/Export Feature**.

The Kadence Hooked Elements feature is a part of the [Kadence Theme Kit Pro](https://www.liquidweb.com/pricing/) plugin.

## Exporting Kadence Elements

To **Export** a **Kadence** **Elements**, head over to the **Dashboard -> Kadence -> Elements** page. You can use the Export All Button at the top of the page to export all of your *Elements* at once. Additionally, you can use the **Checkbox** to select an **Individual** **Element** or a selection of *Elements* you wish to **Export**. Once finished, click on the**Bulk Actions** dropdown, select **Export**, and you will receive a **ZIP download** of your Element(s).

![Exporting Elements](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Exporting-Elements.gif)
## Importing Kadence Elements

You can also **import** **Kadence** **Elements** from the**Dashboard -> Kadence -> Elements** page. Near the top, look for the **Import** button. Then, upload the **ZIP File** that contains the **Kadence** **Elements** and click the **Upload & Import** button.

![Importing Elements](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Importing-Elements.gif)Once finished, you will successfully have imported the **Element** **Content** and **Settings** to your website.
