## How to open the side cart when a product is added to cart

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-open-the-side-cart-when-a-product-is-added-to-cart/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-open-the-side-cart-when-a-product-is-added-to-cart/)

Important! You must first make sure you have added the mini cart to your header and it’s set to show the popout cart for this option to work. [See here.](https://www.liquidweb.com/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-add-a-mini-cart-to-the-header/)

One of the options added by [Kadence Theme Kit Pro](https://kadence-theme.com/premium/)‘s WooCommerce Addons is to slide open an off canvas panel showing the cart items as soon as a product has been added to the cart.

![Add to cart behaviour - Show the cart popout on add to cart](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/kadence-woocommerce-cart-popout-add-to-cart-1024x576.png)To enable this feature, follow these steps:

1. Enable **WooCommerce Addons** at Appearance > Kadence.
2. Click on **Customize** link under WooCommerce Addons or go to Appearance > Customize.
3. Go to **WooCommerce** section and click on Add to **Cart Behavior**.
4. Enable **Show the cart popout on add to cart?** and click **Publish**.
