## How to Decrease WooCommerce Product Prices in Bulk

**Source:** [https://wpsheeteditor.com/decrease-woocommerce-prices-bulk/](https://wpsheeteditor.com/decrease-woocommerce-prices-bulk/)

Want to decrease prices of multiple WooCommerce products at once? WP Sheet Editor lets you filter the products you want to update and decrease their prices by a percentage or a fixed amount in bulk.

For example, you can lower the price of hundreds or thousands of products by 5%, subtract $5 from selected prices, or decrease price only for products in a specific category or price range.

In this tutorial, we’ll show you how to decrease WooCommerce prices in bulk using WP Sheet Editor. You can apply the price reduction to simple products, product variations, specific categories or attributes, and products within a specific price range.

## Decrease WooCommerce prices by percentage or fixed amount

To decrease product prices in bulk, you need to follow two steps: filter for the products you want to update and edit their prices in bulk.

### Step 1. Filter the products you want to update

**Note.** If you’re editing all product prices in your store, skip this step and go straight to Step 2.

The [advanced product search](https://wpsheeteditor.com/advanced-search-woocommerce-products/) tool allows you to filter products by:

- Category
- Attribute
- Product type
- Brand
- Price
- Price range
- SKU
- Other product fields

![decrease-woocommerce-prices-bulk](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/26202026/decrease-prices-by-percentage-simple-products-1.jpg)

In this example, we will edit all the **Simple** products within the **Clothing** category.

In the **Search** tool:

- Enter the product category in the **Enter Product Categories** field.
- Tick the **Enable advanced filters** checkbox and select these values:
- **Field:** Type
- **Operator:** =
- **Value:** Simple
- Click on **Run search**.

![WP Sheet Editor search tool filtering WooCommerce products by category and product type](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/26210057/decrease-prices-in-bulk-5.jpg)

### Step 2. Bulk decrease prices by percentage or fixed amount

After running the search, the spreadsheet will display only the products that match your filters. Now you need to open the **Bulk Edit** tool from the toolbar.

![decrease-woocommerce-prices-bulk](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/26205956/decrease-prices-in-bulk-3.png)

Depending on how you want to decrease prices, select the corresponding values in the **Bulk Edit** tool.

#### Decrease by percentage

To decrease WooCommerce prices by a percentage, you need to filter the products you want to update, open **Bulk Edit**, select the **Regular price** field, choose **Decrease by percentage**, enter the percentage, and click **Execute Now**.

Choose this option if you want to subtract 5% (or any other percentage) from your product prices:

- **Select the rows that you want to update:** Edit all the rows from my current search (including non-visible rows)
- **What field do you want to edit:** Regular price
- **Select type of edit:** Decrease by percentage
- **Decrease by:** In this example, we will decrease prices by **5%**, so enter **5**.
- **Select what you will edit:** Edit only parent products
- Click on **Execute Now**.

![WP Sheet Editor Bulk Edit settings to decrease WooCommerce regular prices by 5 percent](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/26201929/decrease-by-percentage.jpg)

#### Decrease by fixed amount

To decrease WooCommerce prices by a fixed amount, filter the products you want to update, open **Bulk Edit**, select the **Regular price** field, choose **Decrease by number**, enter the amount, and click **Execute Now**.

Choose this option if you want to subtract a $5 (or any other amount) from your product prices:

- **Select the rows that you want to update:** Edit all the rows from my current search (including non-visible rows)
- **What field do you want to edit:** Regular price
- **Select type of edit:** Decrease by number
- **Decrease by:** In this example, we will decrease prices by **$5**, so enter **5**.
- **Select what you will edit:** Edit only parent products
- Click on **Execute Now**.

![WP Sheet Editor Bulk Edit settings to decrease WooCommerce regular prices by 5 dollars](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/26205931/decrease-prices-in-bulk-2.jpg)

## Optional: Decrease prices of product variations

To decrease WooCommerce variation prices in bulk, first filter the products so the spreadsheet shows the variations you want to update. Then open the Bulk Edit tool and select these values:

- **Select the rows that you want to update:** Edit all the rows from my current search (including non-visible rows)
- **What field do you want to edit:** Regular price
- **Select type of edit:** Decrease by number / Decrease by percentage
- **Decrease by:** Enter the number of the percentage or amount
- **Select what you will edit:** Edit only variations
- Click on **Execute Now**.
