## What can you do with AI in WooCommerce?

![List of WooCommerce tasks that can be done with AI, including auditing, writing, SEO, descriptions, titles, alt text, and custom fields](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/21152129/what-can-you-do-with-ai-in-woocommerce-1.jpg)

AI can help you speed up many of the content and catalog tasks that take lots of time when managing a WooCommerce store with many products. You can use it to create new content, update existing product information, and keep your catalog consistent, even when you have hundreds or thousands of products.

- **Create product descriptions:** Generate descriptions for products that need new content using information already in your catalog, such as product names, categories, attributes, dimensions, SKU, etc.
- **Refresh product descriptions:** Rewrite existing descriptions to make them clearer, more useful, and easier to read while keeping the important product details.
- **Improve product content for search:** Update product copy to make it clearer and more relevant to the search terms and topics related to your products.
- **Update SEO information:** Use AI to improve existing [meta titles, meta descriptions, and other SEO content](https://youtu.be/Vm8L3Wms5-M) across older products.
- **Write product meta titles:** Generate search-friendly meta titles in bulk based on your product information and the format you want to use across your store.
- **Generate short descriptions:** Turn product information into concise summaries for product listings, catalogs, and other parts of your store.
- **Organize products with tags:** Generate relevant product tags from information already in your catalog, making it easier to classify and organize products.
- **Generate custom field values:** Fill ACF and other custom fields with product-specific information, such as features, specifications, dimensions, and other details.
- **Translate your catalog:** Translate product titles, descriptions, custom fields, and other catalog information when you start selling to customers in different languages.

## Editing products with AI in a spreadsheet

![screenshot showing how to use ai in spreadsheet cells to edit any woocommerce product field](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/21155821/update-with-ai.jpg)

If you want to generate new content or edit existing content in the cells of the spreadsheet, you can do so in two ways:

#### 1- Using a complete prompt

Click on the cell you want to edit, type 
```
ai:
```

 and then enter the prompt. You can use other columns as context or a basis for generating the content. For example:

```
ai: write a short description by summarizing the $Content$
```

#### 2- Using a global prompt shortcut

That is great when you want to trigger AI prompts on the go. But if you want to run the same prompt for multiple cells, the easy way is to use global prompts and trigger them using a prompt shortcut like:

```
ai:write short description
```

## How to bulk edit WooCommerce product fields with AI

**WP Sheet Editor – AI** lets you use AI prompts to generate or rewrite WooCommerce product fields in bulk. You can apply the same AI instructions to products from your current search or to products you manually select in the spreadsheet.

1. Select the products that you want to edit.
2. Choose the field you want to edit with AI.
3. Enter your prompt or select a global prompt.
4. Preview the result.
5. Generate or rewrite in bulk using the Bulk AI API.

![screenshot showing how to use ai in bulk in woocomemrce](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/21155811/bulk-update-with-ai.jpg)

For large catalogs, test your prompt on a small group of products first. AI output can vary depending on the prompt, provider, and model, so you should verify that the generated content accurately represents your products before applying the same instructions to hundreds or thousands of products.

Also keep API costs in mind. The AI provider you connect to handles the AI billing, so your usage and costs depend on the provider, model, and number of requests you make. Processing a large product catalog can result in significantly more API usage than testing a prompt on a few products.

## What to consider when using AI for WooCommerce

When using AI to generate or rewrite WooCommerce content, always take some time to review and test the results. Make sure your prompt and chosen LLM are producing content that actually fits your products and helps improve your store. AI can save you a lot of time, but you shouldn’t hand over the entire process without reviewing the output and making changes when necessary.

It’s also important to keep API costs in mind. Depending on the AI provider and model you use, each generation or rewrite may use API credits and add to your bill. These costs are charged by your AI provider, not by WP Sheet Editor, so keep an eye on your usage and choose a model and workflow that work within your budget.
