## How to Edit WooCommerce Stock in a Spreadsheet

**Source:** [https://wpsheeteditor.com/how-to-edit-woocommerce-stock-in-a-spreadsheet/](https://wpsheeteditor.com/how-to-edit-woocommerce-stock-in-a-spreadsheet/)

Need to edit WooCommerce stock in a spreadsheet or update stock quantities for multiple products? With **WP Sheet Editor – WooCommerce Products**, you can manage your inventory in a spreadsheet directly inside WordPress.

Instead of opening each product individually, you can:

- **Update stock quantities** for multiple products in one place.
- **Change stock status** to In stock, Out of stock, or On backorder.
- **Enable or disable stock management** for individual products.
- **Edit different products at the same time** and save your changes together.

You don’t need to export products to Excel or upload CSV files. You can edit your existing WooCommerce inventory directly in the spreadsheet.

## 2. Open your WooCommerce products in the spreadsheet

Go to **WP Sheet Editor > Edit Products**.

You’ll see your WooCommerce product catalog in a spreadsheet.

## 3. Edit WooCommerce stock in a spreadsheet

You can now change your inventory information directly in the spreadsheet cells.

Find these three columns:

- **Manage stock:** Enable or disable quantity-based inventory tracking.
- **Stock:** Enter the available stock quantity for each product.
- **Stock status:** Select **In stock**, **Out of stock**, or **On backorder**, as applicable.

![how-to-edit-woocommerce-stock-in-a-spreadsheet](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21010102/woocommerce-stock-columns.png)

### Update stock quantities for multiple products

Find the **Stock** column and enter the new quantity for each product.

For example, you can change one product from 5 to 20 units, another from 12 to 8, and another from 30 to 15.

You can edit as many product rows as needed without leaving the spreadsheet.

If you want WooCommerce to track quantities automatically, make sure the **Manage stock** option is enabled for the relevant products.

### Change WooCommerce stock status

You can also edit the **Stock status** column to change the availability of products that aren’t using quantity-based stock management.

When WooCommerce manages stock quantities automatically, stock status is normally determined by the available quantity and backorder settings.

### Save your inventory changes

After updating your products, click **Save**.

![edit woocommerce stock in a spreadsheet](https://media.wpsheeteditor.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21010400/save-stock-edited-in-spreadsheet.png)

WP Sheet Editor saves your changes to your WooCommerce products. You don’t have to open each product and save it separately.

## Optional: Edit variation stock in the spreadsheet

If you have variable products with variations, you can manage variation stock in the spreadsheet. Just click on **Display variations** to view the variations next to their parent products in the spreadsheet and manage the inventory at the variation level. Forget about editing stock for variations one by one.
