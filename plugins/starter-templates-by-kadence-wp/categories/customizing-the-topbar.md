## Customizing the Topbar

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/customizing-the-topbar/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/customizing-the-topbar/)

From the customizer, open the Header tab and from there you can drag and drop elements into your topbar.

![](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-10.54.56-PM-1024x95-1.png)You can customize the layout, height, and design of your topbar by clicking on the Settings icon beside the Top Row tab as shown here:

![](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-11.02.16-PM.png)![](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-11.05.51-PM.png)![](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1048,h_214,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1777024174/prod/logo-gadget-gaurd/logo-gadget-gaurd.png?_i=AA)All of these settings can be individualized for tablet and mobile devices.
