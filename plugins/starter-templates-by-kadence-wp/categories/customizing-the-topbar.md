## Customizing the Topbar

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/customizing-the-topbar/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/customizing-the-topbar/)

From the customizer, open the Header tab and from there you can drag and drop elements into your topbar.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-10.54.56-PM-1024x95-1.png)

You can customize the layout, height, and design of your topbar by clicking on the Settings icon beside the Top Row tab as shown here:

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-11.02.16-PM.png)

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-11.05.51-PM.png)

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-11.05.59-PM.png)

All of these settings can be individualized for tablet and mobile devices.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-26-at-6.42.29-AM.png)
