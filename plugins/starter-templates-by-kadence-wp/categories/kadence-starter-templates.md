## Installing Kadence Starter Templates (Unlock AI and Pre-Designed Templates)

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/kadence-starter-templates/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/kadence-starter-templates/)

The following steps provide details on installing the **Starter Templates by Kadence** plugin on your WordPress site. This free plugin works strictly with the [Kadence Theme](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/theme/theme-installation/) and allows you to import [pre-designed templates](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/pre-designed-starter-templates/) or personalized [AI-powered designs with Kadence AI](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/kadence-ai-powered-starter-templates/).

![Starter Templates](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Starter-Templates-scaled-e1753992039257.jpg)

[Browse Pre-Designed Kadence Starter Templates](https://www.kadencewp.com/kadence-theme/starter-templates/)

**Premium Templates –**To access *Premium Starter Templates*, your website must meet one of the following requirements:

- The site has the [Kadence Creative Kit](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/kadence-creative-kit/) plugin installed, activated, and licensed. *(Included in the Express Plan or higher.)*
**OR**
- The site has [Kadence Blocks Pro](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/kadence-blocks-pro-plugin/) and the [Kadence Theme Kit Pro](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/theme/theme-kit-pro-plugin/) plugins installed, activated, and licensed using the same license key for both plugins. *(Included in the Plus Plan or higher.)*

Built-in WordPress Installation

You can install the **Starter Templates by Kadence** plugin directly from your WordPress Dashboard. Below are instructions on how you can do this.

In the WordPress Dashboard, go to **Plugins** -> **Add Plugin**

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Add-New-Plugin-scaled-1.png)

Use the search bar in the top right corner and search for**AI Powered Starter Templates by Kadence**. Once it appears, click **Install** **Now**.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Search-Kadence-Starter-Template-Plugin-scaled-1.png)

Then, once you have finished installing the plugin, click on the blue **Activate** button to activate it.

Congratulations! You have successfully installed and activated the Starter Templates by Kadence plugin.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Activate-Kadence-Starter-Template-Plugin.png)

Manual Installation

You can install the Starter Templates by Kadence plugin manually by downloading it from [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/) and uploading it to your WordPress Plugins. Below are instructions on how you can do this.

First, download the AI Powered Starter Templates by Kadence by [clicking here](https://wordpress.org/plugins/kadence-starter-templates/). Be sure to keep the file in its 
```
.zip
```

 format. Do not extract it, as WordPress requires the ZIP for upload.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Download-AI-Powered-Starter-Templates-by-Kadence-WP.png)

Once downloaded, go to your WordPress Dashboard and navigate to **Plugins -> Add Plugin.**

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Add-New-Plugin-scaled-1.png)

At the top of the page, you will now see an **Upload** **Plugin** button. Click on it to upload your ZIP file. You can click the **Choose** **File** button and choose the Kadence Starter Templates ZIP file you downloaded.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Upload-New-Plugin.png)

Once the Plugin File is uploaded, click the **Install** **Now** button

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Upload-New-Plugin-Zip-File-1024x263-1.png)

Then click on the blue **Activate Plugin** button to activate the Starter Templates by Kadence.

Congratulations! You have successfully installed and activated the Starter Templates by Kadence plugin.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Activate-Kadence-Starter-Templates-Plugin-1024x404-1.png)
