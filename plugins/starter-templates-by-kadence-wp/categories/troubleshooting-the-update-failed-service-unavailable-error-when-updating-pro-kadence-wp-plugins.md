## Troubleshooting the “Update Failed: Service Unavailable” error when updating Kadence Pro plugins

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/troubleshooting-the-update-failed-service-unavailable-error-when-updating-pro-kadence-wp-plugins/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/troubleshooting-the-update-failed-service-unavailable-error-when-updating-pro-kadence-wp-plugins/)

If you are seeing the “*Update failed: Service Unavailable*” error while attempting to update your Pro plugins, it may be related to your plugin license. Follow these steps to check your license status and resolve the issue.

## Step 1: Verify Your License Status

1. **Log into Your Account**:
- Log in to your Kadence account *here*.
- Go to the **Downloads & Licenses** page.
2. **See License Information**:

![My Account - Kadence WP 2024-10-31 at 7.10.12 AM](https://p-d0FK22zG.t2.n0.cdn.zight.com/items/qGun8ArK/3a1ca1c6-ec5d-45fa-9a1c-c51176da1f5e.jpg)

## Step 2: Check License Activation and update if necessary in WordPress

There are multiple areas/locations for license activation. Some of these are:

- **Kadence Theme Kit Pro**:
- Go to *Dashboard > Appearance > Kadence*.
- Check the license fields on the top-right of the page.
- **Kadence Blocks Pro**:
- Go to *Dashboard > Kadence > Settings*.
- Check the license fields on the top-right of the page.

*Most users who had this issue haven’t updated their API keys after upgrading to the Lifetime Full Plan.*

## Step 3: Retry the Update

After confirming your license status and making any necessary updates:

1. **Update again**:
- Go back to the **Plugins** section in your WordPress dashboard.
- Update the Pro plugins.

## Additional Support

If the error persists after following these steps, reach out to us by submitting a **premium support ticket**.
