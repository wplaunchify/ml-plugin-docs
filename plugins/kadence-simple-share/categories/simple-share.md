## Kadence Simple Share (Add Social Share Icons to Posts)

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/simple-share/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/simple-share/)

**Kadence Simple Share** provides a lightweight, clean way to add social sharing icons to your WordPress posts, pages, and products. It is intentionally streamlined with no heavy third-party share counts or external tracking APIs to slow down your page load speed just fast, accessible buttons for sharing content across popular social platforms.

## Sharing Options

You can enable different **Sharing** **Sites**. You can pick from: Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, Digg, Tumblr, VK, StumbleUpon, Email, Pinterest, Reddit, XING, or WhatsApp. Just drag a website button from the **Disabled** **Column** over to the **Enabled** **Column** to enable it in the *Social Share Icon List*.

There are five different **Sharing** **Styles** you can choose from. You can select your preferred Sharing Style based on your preferred preview.

![Simple share Settings 1](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Simple-share-Settings-1-901x1024-1.jpg)

The **Sharing** **Size** setting allows you to set a size for your Social Share Icons. This can be set to Normal or Large.

You can use the **Optional Text Before Icons** setting if you’d like to add text before your Social Share Icons.

The **Sharing Placement** setting allows you to set the placement of your Icons. You can pick between: Before content, After Content, Both Before and After Content, Fixed Left, Fixed Right, Fixed Bottom, or None.

You can also set a **Sharing** **Placement** for Mobile.

You can turn the **Transparent** **Background** on or off. 

You can set your icon alignment by using the **Sharing** **Align** setting.

You can enable the Social Share Icons for different Post Types and areas. You can enable this for **Blog** **Post**, **Posts** **Excerpts**, **Pages**, **Portfolio** **Posts** (If applicable), and **Products**. 

You can also enable the **Popup** **Tooltips** **for** **Social** **Links**.

![Simple Share Settings 2](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Simple-Share-Settings-2-626x1024-1.jpg)

You can use the **Tooltip** **Align** setting to align your Tooltip.

You can add a **Tooltip Text before your Social Network Name**.

You can also add a **Tooltip Text before the Email**.

You can **Force Featured Image for Pinterest Share**. This will make the featured image the only image to share on Pinterest.

You can also set the **Load Share Link In** settings. This can be set as a Small Popup Window or in a New Tab.

![Simple Share Settings 3](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Simple-Share-Settings-3-1024x705-1.jpg)

## Kadence Simple Share Shortcode

If you have a **Custom** **Post** **Type** or want to share your icons somewhere other than what is available, you can use the 
```
[kadence_simple_share]
```

 shortcode to do so. You can also just use this shortcode wherever you need. The Kadence Simple Share shortcode can be added to a WordPress Shortcode Block and added to different Post Types or Elements.

![Kadence Simple Share Shortcode](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Kadence-Simple-Share-Demo.jpg)
