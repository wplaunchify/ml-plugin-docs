## Classic Kadence Themes and Plugins

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/classic-themes-and-plugins/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/classic-themes-and-plugins/)

Over the years, Kadence has categorized a number of our legacy products as **Classic** **Themes** and **Plugins**. These products remain supported and maintained to ensure continued compatibility with WordPress and other major plugins. While these classic products no longer receive new feature updates, we provide ongoing maintenance, compatibility fixes, and support for users who continue to rely on them.

Classic Themes and Plugins are available to those who have purchased these products in the past or to users with the [Ultimate Plan](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/pricing) or greater.

**Classic Plugins**: Kadence AMP, Kadence Fullpane Vertical Slider, Kadence Page Transitions, Kadence Pricing Table, Kadence Related Content, Kadence Slider Pro, Kadence Woo Elementor, Kadence Woo Elementor Pro, Kadence Woo SiteOrigin Builder.

**Classic Themes:** Ascend Premium Theme, Pinnacle Premium Theme, Virtue Bold Theme, Virtue Premium Theme.

These are our modern, actively developed tools that include our most up-to-date features and enhancements. For additional premium features, [view our pricing page here](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/pricing). The Classic Themes and Plugins are intended for users who have continued to use them over the years. For new projects, we suggest transitioning to our latest tools to take full advantage of ongoing development and innovation.
