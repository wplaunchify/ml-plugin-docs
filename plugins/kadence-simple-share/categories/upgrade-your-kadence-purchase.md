## How to Upgrade Your License

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/upgrade-your-kadence-purchase/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/upgrade-your-kadence-purchase/)

This article will walk you through the steps involved in upgrading your license.

**To upgrade your license, follow these steps:**

1. Login to your Iconic account here: [https://my.iconicwp.com/](https://my.iconicwp.com/)

![my account login iconic](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/my-account-iconic-1024x532-1.png)

1. If you can’t remember your password, all you’ll need to do is click the ‘Help, I can’t sign in’ link on the login page. Once clicked, you’ll be able to reset your password.
2. Once you’ve logged in, click on the **Subscriptions** link in the left-hand sidebar. Here, you’ll see all your current Iconic plugin subscriptions.

![my account page iconic](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/my-account-page-iconic.png)

1. Click the **Manage** link on the right.

![subscriptions page iconic account area](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/subscriptions-page-iconic-account-area-1024x565-1.png)

1. The next page will give you some more details on your subscriptions, including an **Upgrade or Downgrade** link that you can click to switch your subscription.

![upgrade or renew iconic](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/upgrade-or-renew-iconic-1024x575-1.png)
