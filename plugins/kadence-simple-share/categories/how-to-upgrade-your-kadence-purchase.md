## How to Upgrade Your License

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/how-to-upgrade-your-kadence-purchase/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/how-to-upgrade-your-kadence-purchase/)

This article will walk you through the steps involved in upgrading your license.

**To upgrade your license, follow these steps:**

1. Login to your Iconic account here: [https://my.iconicwp.com/](https://my.iconicwp.com/)

![my account login iconic](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1024,h_532,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1778553359/prod/my-account-iconic/my-account-iconic.png?_i=AA)1. If you can’t remember your password, all you’ll need to do is click the ‘Help, I can’t sign in’ link on the login page. Once clicked, you’ll be able to reset your password.
2. Once you’ve logged in, click on the **Subscriptions** link in the left-hand sidebar. Here, you’ll see all your current Iconic plugin subscriptions.

![my account page iconic](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1002,h_681,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1778553267/prod/my-account-page-iconic/my-account-page-iconic.png?_i=AA)1. Click the **Manage** link on the right.

![subscriptions page iconic account area](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1024,h_565,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1778553218/prod/subscriptions-page-iconic-account-area/subscriptions-page-iconic-account-area.png?_i=AA)1. The next page will give you some more details on your subscriptions, including an **Upgrade or Downgrade** link that you can click to switch your subscription.
