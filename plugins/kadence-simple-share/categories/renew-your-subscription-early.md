## How to Renew Your Subscription Early

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/renew-your-subscription-early/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/renew-your-subscription-early/)

You can Renew your Subscription Early from your [Kadence My Account Subscriptions Page](https://software.liquidweb.com/).

![Subscriptions](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_1024,h_347,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1780355050/prod/Subscriptions_460227cf532/Subscriptions_460227cf532.jpg?_i=AA)When viewing your Subscription Details, under the Auto Renew column, you will see a blue link that says **Renew** **Early**. You can click on it to complete an early renewal.
