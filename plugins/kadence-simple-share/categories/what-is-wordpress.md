---

## WordPress Basics: What You Need to Know

WordPress is one of the most popular platforms for building websites, but it comes in two forms:

### WordPress.com vs. WordPress.org

- **WordPress.com**:- A managed platform where your site is hosted for you.
- Limited customization options unless you’re on higher-tier plans.
- You will need to be on the WordPress Business hosting plan in order to use the Kadence Plugins.
- WordPress.com can be limited depending on the pricing plan that you currently have. Currently, WordPress.com only allows you to use 3rd party themes and plugins if you have a Business or E-Commerce plan. If you have the Personal or Premium plan, you will not be able to upload 3rd party Themes or Plugins to the website.
- **WordPress.org**:- A self-hosted platform that gives you full control over your website.
- Requires a hosting provider where your website lives (like a virtual garage for your files).
- **Kadence is fully compatible** with WordPress.org and is designed to help you build fast, professional sites.

**If you’re unsure which WordPress version you’re using, contact us!** We’re here to help.

---

## What is a WordPress Theme?

A **theme** controls your website’s overall design, including layouts, colors, typography, and styles.

- The Kadence Theme provides a flexible, lightweight foundation for any website.
- Kadence Pro enhances the theme with advanced features like header/footer builders and dynamic content.

👉 [Learn More About the Kadence Theme](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/)

---

## What Are Blocks?

WordPress uses a **block-based editor** (Gutenberg) to design pages and posts. Blocks are individual pieces of content, like text, images, buttons, or galleries.

- **Kadence Blocks** expands Gutenberg with advanced tools to create beautiful, functional layouts.
- Add-ons like Kadence Blocks Pro unlock premium features for enhanced design and functionality.

👉 [Discover Kadence Blocks](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/)

## What is Hosting? Why Do You Need It?

A **hosting provider** is where your website “lives” on the internet. It’s a server that stores your files and makes your site accessible online.

- Excellent customer support for troubleshooting hosting issues.

## What is a Sandbox Site?

A sandbox site is a temporary environment where you can test features, experiment with designs, or demo Kadence tools without affecting a live site.

- **Kadence Free Demo:** A temporary demo site where you can try out Kadence features.- Current limit: 3 hours. We’re exploring ways to extend this timeframe!

---

## What Happens if My License Lapses?

If your Kadence license expires:

- **Your site will continue to function.**
- You’ll lose access to premium features, updates, and support.
- Renewing your license restores access to all features and ensures compatibility with the latest WordPress updates.

## What Happens if I remove Kadence downloads from my site?

- Your site will not function without the Kadence Theme, Kadence Blocks, or other premium tools you may be using.
- Maintaining an active license for Kadence ensures your site’s full functionality and security.

## Order of Operations: Installing Kadence Tools

Setting up Kadence is easy. Install the following in order for a seamless experience:

1. **Kadence Theme (Free)**: Provides the core design foundation.
2. **Kadence Theme Pro**: Unlocks advanced design tools (install as a plugin).
**Kadence Blocks (Free)**: Adds powerful design tools to the block editor.
**Kadence Blocks Pro**: Enhances your layouts with premium features.
