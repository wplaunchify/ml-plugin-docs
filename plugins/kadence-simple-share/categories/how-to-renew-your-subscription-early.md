## How to Renew Your Subscription Early

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/how-to-renew-your-subscription-early/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-general/how-to-renew-your-subscription-early/)

You can Renew your Subscription Early from your [Kadence My Account Subscriptions Page](https://www.kadencewp.com/my-account/subscriptions/).

![Subscriptions](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Subscriptions-1024x347-1.jpg)

When viewing your Subscription Details, under the Auto Renew column, you will see a blue link that says **Renew** **Early**. You can click on it to complete an early renewal.

![Renew Early](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Renew-Early-scaled-1.jpg)
