## Installation

**Source:** [https://quadlayers.com/documentation/woocommerce-autocomplete-orders/installation/](https://quadlayers.com/documentation/woocommerce-autocomplete-orders/installation/)

Here we will explain how to install and activate WooCommerce Autocomplete Orders in your WordPress site.

1. Download the *autocomplete-woocommerce-orders.zip* file from your [account](https://quadlayers.com/account/orders/).
2. Open your WordPress admin panel on *yoursite.com/wp-admin/.*
3. In the WP dashboard, go to **Plugins > Add New**.
4. Click the *Upload Plugin* button, at the top of the screen.
5. Click *Choose File* and use the pop-up window to browse for the *autocomplete-woocommerce-orders.zip* file. Select it.
6. Go to *Plugins > Installed Plugins* and click the **Activate** button under WooCommerce Autocomplete Orders in the plugins list.
