## Integrating Kadence Forms with Kit (ConvertKit)

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/kit-form-adv-block/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/kit-form-adv-block/)

The [Form (Adv) Block](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/advanced-form-block/) supports integration with **Kit (formerly ConvertKit)** when you have [Kadence Blocks Pro](https://www.kadencewp.com/kadence-blocks/pro/) installed. This guide will help you get started with integrating Kadence and Kit.

In order to use the *Kadence/Kit Integration*, you must have the *Kadence Blocks Pro* plugin installed and activated on the website. [You can click here to learn how to get started with Kadence Blocks Pro](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/kadence/kadence-blocks-pro-plugin/).

Kit Setup

To integrate **Kit** with **Kadence Forms**, you must set up a **Kit** account, create a *Form* or *Sequence*, and connect your Kadence Form to Kit with an *API Key*.

Get started by heading over to [Kit](https://kit.com/). You can create a new account by signing up for a free trial or logging into an existing account.

![Kit home page screenshot](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-2.25.05-PM-scaled.png)

Creating a Form

From the Kit Dashboard, click on **Grow**, then select **Landing Pages & Forms**.

![Kit - Select Grow and then Landing Pages & Forms](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-2.45.16-PM.png)

Next, click **+ Create New** and follow the instructions to create a new form.

![Kit - Create New form](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-2.52.30-PM.png)

Creating a Sequence

A Kit Sequence allows you to send multiple emails or trigger additional actions when a new user is added. This feature is only available on paid accounts.

Click **Send** and **Sequences** to add and configure a new Sequence.

![Kit - Create a Sequence](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-2.59.45-PM.png)

Accessing Your API Key

To connect your **Kadence Form (Adv)** to **Kit**, you need a **V3 API Key**. This key enables secure communication between your *Kadence Form* and your *Kit Account*.

From the **Kit dashboard**, click on your **username** on the**top-right bar** and then click **Settings**.

![Kit - Access settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-3.11.37-PM.png)

Once you’re on the **Settings** screen, scroll down and click on the **Developer** menu item. Here, you can access and copy your **V3 API Key**, you’ll need this when setting up your *Kadence Form (Adv) Block*.

![Kit - API Key](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-3.15.50-PM-scaled.png)

Using Kit with the Kadence Form (Adv) Block

Get started by adding a **Form (Adv) Block** to a page.

Navigate to the **Block Settings → General Tab**, expand the **Submit Actions** section, and add **Kit (ConvertKit)** to the list.

![Add Kit to Form (Adv) Submit Actions](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-4.10.44-PM-517x1024-1.png)

Once *Kit* is added, there will be a new **Kit (ConvertKit) Settings** section in the **General Form Block** section.

Expand the **Kit** **Settings** section and enter your **API** **Key**. Once the key is set, click on the **Save** **button** to connect to your *Kit Account.*

You will now be able to select a **Form or Sequence**and also a **Tag** (if you have any set up) from your **Kit** account.

Finally, you’ll need to map your **Kadence Form Fields** to your **Kit Fields**.

Be sure to save your page!

As forms are submitted, the form information will now be sent directly to **Kit** in addition to any other submit actions you may have set up.

![Kadence Kit Settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-06-04-at-3.39.19-PM-433x1024-1.png)

For additional assistance with **Kit**, please see their [Knowledge Base](https://kit.com/support) or reach out to Kit support from your **Kit Dashboard**.
