## What is FacetWP?

FacetWP adds advanced filtering – i.e. *faceted search* – to your result listings.

If you’ve been to any eCommerce site, you’ve probably seen those UI elements (checkboxes, dropdowns, price ranges) that let you narrow down the results. Want to see only “brown shoes priced under $100”? FacetWP does this **really well**.
