# How to give a sample product for free

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, “all-in-one” solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers, extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

This doc explains how to give a free sample product.

1. Go to your WordPress Admin panel. Click on Marketing > Coupons.
2. Create a new coupon with your desired discount type, or open an existing one. Enter the required details.
3. Here we are creating a Fixed Cart discount coupon with a coupon amount of 0. ![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/smart-coupons-general.png?strip=all&w=704)
4. Next, in the Usage limits(tab) > “Usage limit per user” set this to 1, so that the same customer will not be able to place another order using the same coupon. ![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/smart-coupons-usage-limits.png?strip=all&w=704)
5. In the Actions(tab), add the sample product in the “Add products to cart” field with the number of quantities you want to provide as free and add the discount as 100% in the “with discount of” field to provide it as free. ![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/smart-coupons-actions-free-sample.png?strip=all&w=704)
6. Save and publish the coupon.

That’s it!
