# How to link next order coupons to a product

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, “all-in-one” solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

Linking coupons with specific products in WooCommerce can be really helpful. It’s a smart way to offer discounts, [cashbacks](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-offer-cashback-coupons/), or special deals for particular products on their next purchase. This is a great way to encourage repeat purchases and reward your customers with your store.

This documentation explains how to link coupons with a product in the current order so they can be redeemed by customers on their next orders.

## Steps to link coupons to a product

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Go to the **WordPress Admin panel > Marketing > Coupons**.
2. Create coupons by following the steps [here](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-create-coupons-using-smart-coupons/), later (set discount type, restrictions, limits, etc.).
3. Navigate to **Products** and edit the product you want to link with the coupon.
4. Look for ‘**Coupons**’ under the General tab.
5. **Search and select** the coupons (you have created in your store) from the suggestions displayed. **You can link multiple coupons as needed**.![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/coupons-next-order-highlighted.png?strip=all&w=704)
6. Set up all other details for the product.
7. Select **Update** or **Publish** to save changes.

That’s it.

Once a customer purchases this product, all the corresponding coupons linked to this product, either a copy of the coupon or the coupon itself, will be available to the customer.

## How to see linked coupons in the product admin panel

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Go to the **WordPress Admin panel > Products**.
2. Search for the products for which you want to check the linked coupons.
3. You can find the linked coupons under the “**Linked coupons**” column.![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Linked-coupons-highlighted.png?strip=all&w=704)

That’s it. You can see all coupons linked to a product on the product admin panel.

## How to show coupon details to customers

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Your customers can find the coupons linked to the products on the frontend by enabling the following setting:

1. Navigate to the**WooCommerce > Settings**.
2. Open the ‘**Smart Coupons**‘ tab, scroll down to find the ‘**Displaying coupons**‘ section.
3. **Enable** the “**Include coupon details on product’s page, for products that issue coupons**” option.![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Linked-coupons-frontend-highlighted.png?strip=all&w=704)
4. Save the changes.

That’s it. Once enabled, your customer will be able to see what kind of coupons the customer would receive after purchasing that product.
