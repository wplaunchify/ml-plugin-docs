# Use Case: How to offer bonus Gift Certificates with Gift Card purchases

			## Overview

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Offering bonus Gift Certificates is a great way to increase product sales while encouraging repeat purchases.

With Smart Coupons, you can automatically reward customers with a bonus Gift Certificate when they purchase a Gift Card product.

For example, when a customer purchases a $300 Gift Card, they will receive:

- A **$300 Gift Certificate**
- An additional **$30 Bonus Gift Certificate**

Both Gift Cards are generated uniquely and emailed to the customer once the order is completed. You can even restrict the Gift Certificate to specific product categories, such as **electronics, fashion,** etc., helping drive sales in targeted areas of your store.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Gift-Card-Bonus-Banner.jpg?w=980)

## Business Scenario

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

A store owner wants to create a promotional campaign where customers purchasing a Gift Card receive bonus Gift Certificates for future purchases.

**Example Promotion**:

**Buy a $300 Gift Card and receive an additional $30 bonus Gift Certificate.**

- Receive a **$300 Gift Certificate.**
- Receive an additional **$30 bonus Gift Certificate.**
- A bonus Gift Certificate can be redeemed only on **Electronics products.**

This promotion increases Gift Card purchases today while generating future sales in a selected product category.

## How it Works with Smart Coupons

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Smart Coupons allows you to:

- Create Store Credit / Gift Certificate coupons
- Restrict Gift Certificate redemption to specific product categories
- Link multiple coupons to a single product
- Automatically generate unique coupon codes
- Email Gift Certificates to customers after order completion

Once the qualifying purchase is completed:

1. Smart Coupons generates both Gift Certificates ($300 + $30 bonus).
2. The customer receives the Gift Certificate codes via email.
3. The Gift Certificates can be redeemed on future purchases according to the configured restrictions.

## Steps to set up

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

### Step 1: Create the first Gift Certificate coupon

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Go to **Marketing > Coupons > Add New**.
2. Create a coupon.
3. Set **Discount Type** > Store Credit / Gift Certificate.
4. Set the coupon value to **$300**
5. Publish the coupon.

### Step 2: Create the bonus Gift Certificate coupon

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Go to **Marketing > Coupons > Add New**again.
2. Create another coupon.
3. Set **Discount Type** > Store Credit / Gift Certificate.
4. Set the coupon value to **$30**.
5. (Optional) Under **Usage restriction**, tab restrict to specific categories such as **Electronics**.
6. Publish the coupon.

### Step 3: Link the coupons to your product

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Create / Edit the Gift Card product customers will purchase.
2. Link both Gift Certificate coupons to the product.
3. Save or update the product. For more details to link coupons to products, you can refer to the documentation [here](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-give-coupons-with-products-for-next-order/).

## Customer Experience

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Customer purchases a **$300 Gift Card.**
2. The order is completed.
3. Smart Coupons generates a $300 Gift Certificate coupon and a $30 bonus Gift Certificate coupon.
4. Both unique coupon codes are emailed to the customer.
