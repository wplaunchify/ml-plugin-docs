# How to display the product’s original and discounted prices in the cart

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, “all-in-one” solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers, extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

This doc article explains how to display the product’s original and discounted prices in the cart.

## How to enable display of discounted prices in the cart

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Go to your **WordPress Dashboard**.
2. Navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings** > **Smart Coupons** tab.
3. Under the **General** section, look for the option **Display Discounted Price**.![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Strikethrough-Price.png?strip=all&w=704)
4. Enable this setting.
5. Click **Save changes**.

## How this appears on the cart page:

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

When a discount coupon is applied, customers will see:

- The **original product price** will appear with a strike-through price.![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Strikethrough-Smart-Coupons.png?strip=all&w=704)
- This makes discounts more visible and improves customer confidence during checkout.

That’s it.
