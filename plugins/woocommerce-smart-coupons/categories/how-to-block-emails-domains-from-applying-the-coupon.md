# How to block emails or domains from applying the coupon

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, all-in-one solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

To avoid coupon misuse, loss or spam, it is advisable to block certain emails or domains from applying the coupon.

This documentation explains how you can block specific emails or domains from doing the same.

## Steps to block specific emails or domains

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. From your store’s WP Admin dashboard, navigate to *Marketing > Coupons*.
2. Create a new coupon or open an existing one. Enter the required details.
3. Navigate to the **Usage restriction** tab. Scroll down to find the **Smart Coupons Restrictions** dropdown field, select the **Exclude emails** option, and click on the **Add** button.
4. Add the necessary emails (comma-separated) you want to block. For example, to enter multiple emails, enter 
```
john@example.com, sam@example.com
```

. 

To block all emails of a specific domain, enter the domain name and prefix it with an 
```
@
```

 — for instance, 
```
@xyz.com
```

. ![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/excluded-restriction.png?strip=all&w=704)

That’s it!

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/smart-coupons-blocked-emails-to-use-coupon.png?strip=all&w=704)

**Note**: Similarly, you can also [exclude user roles](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-restrict-coupon-by-user-roles/) or [exclude product attributes](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-restrict-a-coupon-based-on-product-attributes/) from applying the coupon.
