# How to Find Coupons Restricted by Email Address

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, “all-in-one” solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

This doc article explains how using Smart Coupons, you can find or search coupons that are restricted by email address.

## Steps to find email restricted coupons

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/smart-coupons-email-restricted-coupons.png?strip=all&w=704)

1. Go to your WordPress Admin panel. Click on **Marketing > Coupons**.
2. Look for the “**Search coupons**“ button at the top right.
3. In the search bar, first enter the prefix ‘**email:**’. This is required to define what you’re searching for, in the email restriction or Allowed email field of the coupons.
4. Now enter the email address after ’email:’. For example: “**email: john@example.com**”.
5. Click on the “**Search coupons**” button.

All coupons restricted for that email address will appear.
