# How to restrict coupons by payment methods

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, “all-in-one” solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

Smart Coupons also provides advanced coupon restrictions based on payment methods, [location](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-restrict-coupon-by-country-state-city-and-zip-code/), [shipping methods](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-restrict-coupon-by-shipping-method/) and others.

This doc article explains how you can restrict the usage of a coupon to specific payment methods.

Some customers prefer only trusted/popular or their local payment gateway while making a purchase. Restricting coupons to these payment methods will encourage your customers to buy from you.

## How to configure payment-based coupons?

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Navigate to **WordPress Admin panel** > **Marketing > Coupons**.
2. Create a coupon or edit an existing coupon.
3. In the “**Usage restriction**” tab, under the **Smart Coupons Restrictions** dropdown field, select “**Payment methods**” and then click on the “**Add**” button.
4. **Search and select** the payment method(s) you want to allow for this coupon. In this example, if you select **PayPal** or **Stripe**, the coupon will only apply when the customer chooses one of these payment options.

![Smart Coupons Payments](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Smart-Coupons-Payment.png?strip=all&w=704)

1. Click **Publish** or **Update** to save your changes.

That’s it!

**Note**: You can also club location and payment method restrictions for subscription coupons. For example, apply a 15% off recurring product discount coupon for six months only for customers in Europe and for payment method PayPal.
