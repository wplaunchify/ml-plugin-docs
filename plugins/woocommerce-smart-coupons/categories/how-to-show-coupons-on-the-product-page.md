# How to show coupons on the product page

			Smart Coupons provide a powerful, “all-in-one” solution for gift cards, store credits, discount coupons, and vouchers, extending the core functionality of WooCommerce coupons.

This doc article explains how to show coupons on the product page using the shortcode on your WooCommerce store.

## Steps to show coupons on the product page

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

1. Navigate to your WordPress admin panel.
2. Go to **Products > All Products**.
3. Edit the product for which you want to show the coupons.
4. Copy the shortcode “
```
[wc_sc_available_coupons]
```

 ” and paste it in the **Product short or long description** where you’d like to display the coupons. ![Smart Coupons Product description](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Smart-Coupons-Product-description.png?strip=all&w=704)
5. Click the **Update** button to save the changes.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Smart-Coupons-Beanie_Short_Code.png?strip=all&w=704)

That’s it!

The available coupons will now be displayed on the product page.

**Note:**Only the coupons that are valid and match the Usage restrictions will be shown on the page. You can also display coupons based on the [category of the coupons](https://woocommerce.com/document/smart-coupons/how-to-display-available-coupons-on-any-page-using-shortcode/#section-2).
