## How to Add a Mini Cart to the Site Header

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-add-a-mini-cart-to-the-header/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/how-to-add-a-mini-cart-to-the-header/)

When WooCommerce is installed and active in your Kadence-powered WordPress site, you can easily add a functional mini cart in your site’s header.

This feature is available in the free Kadence theme and does not require Kadence Theme Kit Pro.  However, with [Kadence Theme Kit Pro](https://www.kadencewp.com/pricing/), you can also add a [free shipping notice](#free-shipping-cart-notice) to your mini cart.

By default, the cart icon will link to your WooCommerce cart page.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/header-cart-icon-1024x147-1.png)

With Cart Click Action set to POPOUT CART, clicking the cart icon will slide open the mini cart from the side of the screen.

To add a mini cart to your header go to **Customizer > Header** and drag **Cart** item to your desired position in the header builder.

![Adding Header Cart to Header Builder](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/header-mini-cart-kadence-1024x576-1.jpg)

To get to the settings of Cart item, click on the pencil icon for the cart item in the header or the gear icon in the header builder.

![Editing Cart item settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/editing-cart-item-settings-1024x576-1.jpg)

Free Shipping Cart Notice (Pro Feature)

When the **Cart Click Action** is set to **Popout Cart** or **Dropdown Cart**, you can also enable the free shipping cart notice.  This feature is only available with Kadence Theme Kit Pro.

**Amount needed for Free Shipping:**  Set the minimum cart value needed for free shipping.  Note this does not enable free shipping but simply displays the notice when the cart total is less than this value.

**Cart Notice Message:**  Set the message to display when the user has not met the free shipping threshold.  Use 
```
{cart_difference}
```

 to display the amount needed to qualify.
