## Social Media Icons in the Header

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/social-media-icons-header/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/social-media-icons-header/)

To add your social network links, navigate to General > Social Links from the customizer.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-10.13.51-AM-585x1024-1.png)

Next you can add social links to your header and footer.

You can customize the links by clicking on the Settings icon, as shown here:

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-10.19.55-AM.png)

From there, you can customize the styling of your icons and choose whether or not you want to show the icon label.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2020-06-25-at-10.22.45-AM-368x1024-1.png)
