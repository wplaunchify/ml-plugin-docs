## Edit Dropdown Menu Styling

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/edit-dropdown-menu-styling/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-theme/edit-dropdown-menu-styling/)

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/kadence-dropdown-menu-01-300x268-1.jpg)

Within Kadence you can control the dropdown menu background and font styles from within the customizer. Start by going to appearance > customize. Then click “header” under the header settings you will see one of your navigation areas. For example: “primary navigation”. Click on that then in that section at the bottom you can see the link for the “dropdown options”.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/kadence-dropdown-menu-02.jpg)

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/kadence-dropdown-menu-03-522x1024-1.jpg)

Once in the dropdown options you can edit the general and design settings.
