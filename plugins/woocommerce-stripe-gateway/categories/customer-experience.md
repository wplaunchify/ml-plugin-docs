## Does the Stripe extension support 3D Secure?

**Source:** [https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/3d-secure/](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/3d-secure/)

# Does the Stripe extension support 3D Secure?

			Yes, the Stripe extension supports 3D Secure (also known as “3DS” or “SCA”) on both the checkout page and [the “Pay for order” page](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-orders/paying-for-orders/).

If you wish to test the 3D Secure checkout flow on your store, you can use one of the [3D Secure test cards](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#regulatory-cards) with the Stripe extension in [test mode](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/testing/).

# How will additional payment methods appear on my checkout page?

			Merchants who use the Stripe extension can also offer [additional payment methods](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/setup-and-configuration/additional-payment-methods/) to customers. These additional payment methods will be displayed as if they are separate payment gateways, in addition to the usual card payment method.

The extension now offers the Optimized Checkout Suite to use machine learning to decide the order in which payment methods should be displayed to individual shoppers, with the goal of prioritizing payment methods that are most likely to lead to purchases.

## Does the Stripe extension support Cartes Bancaires?

**Source:** [https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/cartes-bancaires/](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/cartes-bancaires/)

# Does the Stripe extension support Cartes Bancaires?

			The Stripe extension does support [co-badged](https://docs.stripe.com/co-badged-cards-compliance) cards (e.g. [Cartes Bancaires](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/cartes-bancaires)) by allowing customers to choose their preferred card brand during checkout.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-07-23-at-13.41.10-UTC@2x.png?w=980)

Note that customers will only be able to choose their preferred card brand if the following requirements are met:

- Your Stripe account is located in [the European Economic Area (EEA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Area).
- Your Stripe account is at least three days old.
- Your [store currency](https://woocommerce.com/document/shop-currency/) is set to EUR.
- The customer’s cart total is at least €50 EUR. (This requirement does not apply if the country of your Stripe account is set to France.)

If you want to test the co-badged card checkout process on your store, you can use a [co-badged test card](https://docs.stripe.com/co-badged-cards-compliance#testing) while the Stripe extension is in [test mode](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/testing/).

## What is the checkout experience like with the Stripe extension?

**Source:** [https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/checkout/](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/checkout/)

# What is the checkout experience like with the Stripe extension?

			The Stripe for WooCommerce extension offers two checkout options: the standard checkout experience and **Optimized Checkout Suite**. Both allow customers to complete purchases using payment methods from Stripe, but they differ in design, flexibility, and optimization features.

## Overview

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

In WooCommerce, customers can complete purchases using payment methods embedded into the site’s checkout page. [Optimized Checkout Suite](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/admin-experience/optimized-checkout-suite/) is our newest checkout experience that’s fully optimized for conversion.

It includes:

- Streamlined and responsive card payment fields
- Any [additional payment methods](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/additional-payment-methods/) you’ve enabled in your settings
- AI models that dynamically display the most relevant payment methods based on the shopper’s behavior and preferences.
- Optional [Adaptive Pricing](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/admin-experience/optimized-checkout-suite/adaptive-pricing/) controls, which let shoppers pay in their preferred currency

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-04-27-at-14.17.38-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

If the standard checkout experience is enabled, customers will see:

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-04-27-at-14.19.20-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

## How are saved payment methods handled?

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

The Stripe extension [includes an option](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/setup-and-configuration/settings-guide/#payments-and-transactions) that allows customers to [securely save their payment method](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/saved-payment-information/#security) to their account. This is great if you want to allow customers to quickly use the same details to make a purchase on your site in the future.

For customers who have a saved card, they are presented with the stored card options on file, or they can [save a new one](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/saved-payment-information/#checkout).

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-04-27-at-14.21.24-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

## How can customers use express checkouts?

**Source:** [https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/express-checkouts/](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/express-checkouts/)

# How can customers use express checkouts?

			When express checkouts are enabled, customers can see those buttons on the product page, cart page, and/or checkout page when using a supported device, browser, and payment method.

The Stripe extension is compatible with four express checkout options:

- [Amazon Pay](https://pay.amazon.com/)
- [Apple Pay](https://www.apple.com/apple-pay/)
- [Google Pay](https://pay.google.com/about/)
- [Link by Stripe](https://link.com/)

**Note:** A good way to check if your or your user’s device is compatible is to ask them visit [https://stripe-payments-demo.appspot.com/](https://stripe-payments-demo.appspot.com/) This is Stripe test site, if they don’t see express checkout buttons there, then their device or browser is not compatible or doesn’t have a payment method added.

## Apple Pay requirements

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

In order for Apple Pay to display, the customer must:

- Use [a compatible device](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102896).
- Be [logged in](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204053) with their Apple ID.
- Have [a payment method](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT201266) added their Apple ID.
- View your site on [a compatible browser](https://stripe.com/docs/elements/express-checkout-element#supported-browsers).

## Google Pay requirements

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

In order for Google Pay to display, the customer must:

- View your site with [a compatible browser](https://stripe.com/docs/elements/express-checkout-element#supported-browsers).
- Be [logged into their Google account](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/185277?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop) via their browser.
- Have [a payment method](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/9244912?hl=en) added to their Google account.

## Amazon Pay requirements

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

In order for Google Pay to display, the customer must:

- View your site with [a compatible browser](https://pay.amazon.com/help/202030010).

## Link by Stripe

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Most customers are already familiar with how Apple Pay and Google Pay work, but Link by Stripe is a newer express checkout method, and it works a bit differently.

While it does show an express checkout button similar to those for Apple Pay and Google Pay, Link by Stripe is also integrated directly into the card payment form.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-12-12-at-20.28.20-UTC.gif)

If Links detects that the customer has entered an email belonging to a Link account (either in the card payment form or in the billing details form) it will automatically send an SMS two-factor code to the phone number on file for that account.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-12-12-at-20.25.34-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

By entering the code into the Link box, your customer can easily check out with their payment method on file with Link.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-12-12-at-20.32.18-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

Link accounts will also be saved to customer accounts on your site, so customers can see them under the **My Account** page.

## Product compatibility

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

We’ve added some information below about product types that are fully compatible, partially compatible, and incompatible with express checkout methods via the Stripe extension.

### Fully compatible

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Both [simple and variable products](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-products/#product-types) are supported with express checkouts.

### Partially compatible

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Express checkouts currently have added compatibility for products created with the following extensions:

- [WooCommerce Pre-Orders](https://woocommerce.com/products/woocommerce-pre-orders/) only supports express checkout buttons if the pre-order products are configured to charge customers immediately. Pre-order products charged upon release are not supported by express checkout methods.
- [WooCommerce Subscriptions](https://woocommerce.com/products/woocommerce-subscriptions/) supports express checkout buttons on all pages for simple and variable subscription products for customers who are [logged into your store](https://woocommerce.com/document/the-my-account-page/). It also supports using some of the Stripe plugin’s express checkout methods on the change payment method page for pre-existing subscriptions.

Some products require advanced configuration to determine their price and availability. In such instances, express checkouts are only compatible using the cart and checkout pages. Examples of such plugins are:

- [Composite Products](https://woocommerce.com/products/composite-products)
- [Product Bundles](https://woocommerce.com/products/product-bundles/)
- [WooCommerce Bookings](https://woocommerce.com/products/woocommerce-bookings/)
- [Grouped Products](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-products/#create-grouped-product)

### Incompatible

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Known incompatibilities with express checkout buttons include but are not limited to:

- [External/Affiliate Products](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-products/#adding-an-external-affiliate-product)
- [All Products for Woo Subscriptions](https://woocommerce.com/products/all-products-for-woocommerce-subscriptions/)
- [Product Add-Ons](https://woocommerce.com/products/product-add-ons/)
- Express checkout buttons do not support custom product fields such as Product Add-Ons on single product pages.
- To avoid orders being placed with no add-ons paid, you can disable express checkouts on single product pages.

## Testing the Stripe extension

**Source:** [https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/testing/](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/testing/)

# Testing the Stripe extension

			The Stripe extension is very versatile. A part of this versatility is a dedicated way to perform test transactions on your WooCommerce site via the built-in test mode feature. This allows you to make purchases using [Stripe test cards](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#cards).

Performing test transactions while in test mode is a great way to simulate the customer experience or [troubleshoot issues](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/troubleshooting/) without having to charge real payment methods.

In this guide, you will learn how to configure the Stripe extension to enable test mode and accept test payments.

## Configuring the Stripe extension for testing

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

When you initially [connect the Stripe extension to your Stripe account](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/setup-and-configuration/connecting-to-stripe/), it only connects in live mode. Connecting to your Stripe account in test mode is a separate process. To connect in test mode:

1. Go to **WooCommerce > Settings > Payments** tab.
2. Click the **Manage** button for the Stripe payment method.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2025-02-14-at-09.37.31-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

1. Click the **Settings** tab in the Stripe settings page.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-08-08-at-18.41.19-UTC@2x.png?w=980)

1. In the *Account details* section, click the **Configure connection** button.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-08-08-at-18.42.58-UTC@2x.png?w=980)

1. Click the **Test** tab to view the connection status of test mode.
- If you haven’t configured test mode yet, the account status will say “Disconnected.”
2. Click the **Create or connect a test account** button.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Screenshot-taken-on-2025-10-09-at-13.58.35-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

1. You will be prompted to connect to your Stripe account in test mode. Click **Connect** to do so.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2025-02-14-at-09.41.27-UTC@2x.png?w=980)

1. You’ll be redirected back to the Stripe settings page in your WordPress dashboard.
2. Click the **Configure connection** button in the *Account details*section again.
3. Confirm that the connection to your account’s test mode is working.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Screenshot-taken-on-2025-10-09-at-13.33.52-UTC@2x-1.png?strip=all&w=704)

Now that you’ve connected to your Stripe account’s test mode, you can [enable test mode](#enabling) and start using test cards.

## Enabling test mode

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

After you’ve [connected to Stripe in test mode](#configuring), you can enable test mode in the Stripe extension like so:

1. Navigate to **WooCommerce > Settings > Payments > Stripe > Settings**.
2. Check the **Enable test mode** option in the *General* section.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-06-16-at-08.13.29-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

1. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Save Changes**.

## Using test mode

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Once [test mode is enabled](#enabling), you can make a test purchase by:

1. Navigating to your store’s shop page.
2. Adding a product to your cart.
3. Proceeding to the checkout page.
4. Adding a [Stripe test card](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#cards) as a payment method.
- There are numerous cards for testing different scenarios, such as successful payments, card declines, disputes, etc.
- Some payment methods may show basic test values you can use in the checkout form itself.
5. Clicking the **Place order** button.
6. If you used a card that resulted in a successful payment, you’ll be redirected to the “Order received” page just as if the transaction were real.

After completing a successful test payment, you can view its details in your Stripe dashboard by clicking the linked Charge ID beneath the order number at the top of the edit order page for your testing order.

![Beneath the order number on the "Edit Order" page of your testing order, you'll find the Charge ID for your test payment, which can be clicked to access the payment details on your Stripe dashboard.](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/WooCommerce-Stripe-link-to-dashboard.png?strip=all&w=704)Click the linked charge ID on your testing order to view its details in the Stripe dashboard.

### Using test mode with express checkouts

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

You can also test the Stripe extension’s [express checkout methods](https://woocommerce.com/document/stripe/customer-experience/express-checkouts/) while in test mode.

For Google Pay, you’ll need to add one of the [Stripe test cards](https://docs.stripe.com/testing#cards) to [your Google account](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/9244912?hl=en). If you’re testing Apple Pay, you simply use your real card information while in test mode. This of course will **not** actually place a charge on your card.

To test Amazon Pay, you will need to create a test buyer account for their sandbox environment as instructed [in Amazon’s documentation](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/amazon-pay-checkout/amazon-pay-sandbox-accounts.html).

To test Link by Stripe, follow the instructions [here](https://github.com/woocommerce/woocommerce-gateway-stripe/wiki/Critical-flows-%E2%80%90-Instructions#checkout-with-link).

### Using test mode with SEPA and other non-card payments

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Similar to credit card payments, SEPA and other non-card payments can be tested using test data at checkout while in test mode. Mock payment information will appear at checkout while in test mode for supported payment methods. [Stripe has specific payment method test data](https://docs.stripe.com/testing?payment-method=sepa-direct-debit#non-card-payments) for different scenarios in their documentation.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-06-03-at-14.54.59-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

## Stripe.js testing assistant

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

The Stripe.js file, which our Stripe plugin uses, includes [a testing assistant/widget](https://docs.stripe.com/sdks/stripejs-testing-assistant) that can be configured to appear on the checkout page in test mode. When enabled, the testing assistant provides tools that allow you to:

- Autofill fields with test data for various scenarios
- Highlight errors, warnings, and optimizations
- Simulate potential customer locations
- Customize the form appearance in real time

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-06-03-at-14.56.28-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

To enable the testing assistant, use this code snippet:

```
add_filter( 'wc_stripe_show_stripe_developer_widget', function ( $show_widget ) {
	$current_user = wp_get_current_user();
	$current_login = $current_user ? $current_user->user_login : null;

	WC_Stripe_Logger::debug( 'wc_stripe_show_stripe_developer_widget', [
		'show_widget' => $show_widget,
		'current_username' => $current_login,
	] );
	
	if ( 'YOUR_USERNAME_HERE' === $current_login ) { // feel free to use another username
		return true;
	}

	return $show_widget;
}, 10, 1 );
```

Replace 
```
YOUR_USERNAME_HERE
```

 with any username from your site that you want to be able to see the testing assistant on the checkout page.

See [the Stripe.js testing assistant page](https://docs.stripe.com/sdks/stripejs-testing-assistant) of Stripe’s documentation for more information about how you can use the assistant itself.

## Connecting to a test only Stripe account

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

If you’ve created a Stripe account but not [set it up](https://docs.stripe.com/get-started/account/set-up) yet (by entering your business information), you may wind up with a test-only account that looks like this:

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-08-07-at-19.16.19-UTC@2x.png?w=980)

If you’d like to connect our Stripe plugin to this account, you can do so by using the **Create or connect a test account instead** link on the connection screen.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-08-07-at-19.20.52-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

You’ll be taken to Stripe.com to connect, but since your account is test-only, you can simply click the **Skip this form** button.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2024-08-07-at-19.22.21-UTC@2x.png?strip=all&w=704)

Doing so will return you to the WordPress dashboard with the Stripe plugin already in test mode and connected to your test-only Stripe.com account.

You can click the **Configure connection** button and then select the **Test** tab to verify that the plugin is working correctly.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Screenshot-taken-on-2025-10-09-at-13.33.52-UTC@2x-3.png?strip=all&w=704)

Note that as long as your Stripe account is restricted to test mode only, you will not be able to disable test mode in the Stripe plugin settings.

![](https://woocommerce.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Screenshot-taken-on-2026-06-16-at-08.32.47-UTC@2x.png?w=980)
