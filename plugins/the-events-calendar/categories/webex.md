## Creating Virtual Events with Webex

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/webex/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/webex/)

With Events Calendar Pro, you can connect to your Webex account and add your pre-existing Webex meetings to your events.

#### With The Events Calendar and WordPress you can:

- Generate Webex meetings from your WordPress dashboard in The Events Calendar plugin.
- Add existing Webex meetings by simply pasting the meeting URL into the Smart URL field in your event settings.
- Allow users to easily connect to your Webex meetings through your event pages created in The Events Calendar.

## Connect Your Site to Your Webex Account

1. Navigate to **Events → Settings → Integrations** from the WordPress dashboard.
2. Find the Webex Connection and click **Add Webex Account**.
3. You will be redirected to Webex, where you can log in or create an account.
4. From there, you can go back to your site and see that it says Refresh Connection, which means that you are already connected.
5. Start automatically generating and adding your pre-existing Webex meetings!

![Webex meeting](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screen-Shot-2022-03-25-at-7.14.50-AM.jpg)

### Adding Multiple Accounts

You have the ability to add multiple Webex accounts to your site. This way, you can create Webex from different Webex accounts at the same time on your site. If you’d like to add multiple Webex accounts, you must first log out of one account in [Webex](https://www.webex.com/) before logging in to another Webex account on your site.
