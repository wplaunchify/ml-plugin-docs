## Calendar and Ticket Shortcodes

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/shortcodes-2/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/shortcodes-2/)

Easily embed event content across your website with shortcodes, even if you’re not a coder. Use this guide to get the lowdown on all of The Events Calendar’s shortcodes for displaying your calendar, event details, tickets, widgets, and more. Shortcodes also make it simple to use The Events Calendar with page builder plugins like Divi, Elementor, or Beaver Builder.

Events Calendar Pro

Take events beyond the calendar. Use shortcodes and widgets to embed calendar content or single events on your posts and pages.

- [Using The Events Calendar Shortcodes](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/shortcodes/)
- [Add Event Content to Event Pages with Shortcodes](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/single-event-shortcode/)

Event Tickets Plus

Embed ticket content for prospective or registered attendees, or embed an attendee list to publicly display who will attend an event.

- [How to Show Event Content Only to Attendees](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/protected-content/)
- [Embedding Tickets, RSVPs, and Attendees with Shortcodes](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/tickets-shortcodes/)

Community Events

Shortcodes for sharing your crowdsourced event information, including event submission forms and sales reports.

- [How to Use Community Events Shortcodes](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/community-shortcodes/)

Page Builders

Add our shortcodes directly inside your favorite page builder, like Divi, Elementor, or Beaver Builder.

- [Using Elementor Widgets with The Events Calendar](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/elementor-widgets/)
- [Using the Divi Theme with The Events Calendar](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/divi-theme/)
