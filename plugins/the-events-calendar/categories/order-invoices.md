## Finding Order Invoices

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/order-invoices/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/order-invoices/)

If you’ve purchased a premium plugin from us and need an invoice, we’ve got you covered. First, go to your account page and [Account Details](https://theeventscalendar.com/my-account/) screen in your account. Scroll down to the **Transaction History** section and find the transaction for which you need the invoice. Once you’ve found the transaction, click on the three dots next to it and select **Invoice** from the dropdown menu.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-01-27-at-22.46.15-1024x331-1.jpg)

The invoice will open in a new tab. You can then download it by using the **Download** button. The appearance and location of the download option may vary depending on your browser.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-01-27-at-22.52.07.jpg)

### Adding a VAT number

If you need a specific VAT number to appear on your invoice, you can add it under Payment Methods > Edit Address >VAT number on your Account page.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Screenshot-2025-01-27-at-23.01.15-1024x728-1.jpg)
