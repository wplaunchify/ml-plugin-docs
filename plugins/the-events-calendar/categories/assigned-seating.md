## The Events Calendar Assigned Seating

**Source:** [https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/events-calendar/assigned-seating/](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/events-calendar/assigned-seating/)

The Assigned Seating add-on for Event Tickets transforms how venue managers and event coordinators sell tickets on WordPress. Ideal for limited-capacity venues like theaters, auditoriums, and banquet halls, this powerful tool allows attendees to preview available space and select their exact seats prior to checkout. Shifting from a general admission structure to a structured seating system helps maximize ticket tier revenue, streamline on-site check-ins, and significantly improve the overall buying experience for your customers.

Operating this add-on requires connecting your WordPress site directly to the Seating Builder service, a cloud-based SaaS tool designed specifically to manage intricate maps and physical layouts without draining your local server resources. Once licensed, site administrators can build customized, multi-tier seating charts and seamlessly map those physical spots to specific e-commerce tickets. On the front end, customers interact with an intuitive selection modal, which site owners can visually style or modify with custom CSS alerts, policies, or booking time limits. The system also includes dedicated troubleshooting resources for handling common API verification issues, ensuring your ticket sales platform remains completely automated and functional.
