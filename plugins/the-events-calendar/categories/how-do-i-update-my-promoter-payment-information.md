## Updating Payment Information

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/how-do-i-update-my-promoter-payment-information/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/how-do-i-update-my-promoter-payment-information/)

All payment information and billing records can be found in your [TheEventsCalendar.com account](https://theeventscalendar.com/my-account/).

Log in with the email address and password you created when purchasing your subscription and then navigate to the [Payment Methods](https://theeventscalendar.com/my-account/payment-methods/) screen to add, edit and remove payment options.
