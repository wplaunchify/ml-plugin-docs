## Do You Provide Discounts if I Purchase Multiple Products?

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/bundle-discounts/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/bundle-discounts/)

The quick answer: **Yes!**

The detailed answer is that we offer discounts on [products we’ve bundled together](https://theeventscalendar.com/bundles/), where you can save up to 25% off the package.

These are the bundles we currently offer:

- [Events Marketing Bundle](https://theeventscalendar.com/bundles/event-promoter-bundle/)
- [Events Importer Bundle](https://theeventscalendar.com/bundle-importer)
- [Community Manager Bundle](https://theeventscalendar.com/bundle-community-manager)
- [Ultimate Bundle](https://theeventscalendar.com/bundle-ultimate)

The discount will be applied when adding the bundle to your cart.
