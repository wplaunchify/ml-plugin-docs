## Trial licenses

While we do not have an official “trial license” or “trial period,” we *do* have [a 30-day, no-questions-asked refund policy](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/refund-policy/) that many folks use to facilitate a “trial period.”

This refund policy, [which you can read about here](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/refund-policy/), lets you tinker with the premium add-ons for up to thirty days. We process refunds in usually no more than one business day.
