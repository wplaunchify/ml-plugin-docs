## Accessing Support

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/accessing-support/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/accessing-support/)

Every active license subscription for The Events Calendar products comes with prioritized support from our team. But even if you do not have an active license for our products, there are still ways to get support.

[Contact Support](https://theeventscalendar.com/support/#contact)

## Active licenses

If you have an active license, you have direct access to ask our support team questions. To contact the support team, [visit our Support page and submit the form](https://theeventscalendar.com/support/#contact). Note, for premium support you will need to [log in to your account](https://theeventscalendar.com/my-account/) first.

Remember, the more details you provide, the better we can do to support you. And if you’re running into a specific issue, it’s also helpful if you [run through these steps](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/testing-for-conflicts/) and [share your system information](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/sharing-your-system-information/) so we can jump right in.

## Free support

### WordPress.org support forums

The support forums on WordPress.org are another place to seek help. While our support does monitor and occasionally respond to questions in the forum, this is more of a space for you to connect with other people using our products. The forums have been around for many years, so they’re also a great place to search for questions and answers that may be exactly what you’re looking for.

You can find the forums here:
