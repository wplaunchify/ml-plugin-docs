## The Events Calendar Compliance

**Source:** [https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/events-calendar/compliance/](https://www.liquidweb.com/help-docs/software/events-calendar/compliance/)

When selling tickets and gathering attendee information, maintaining strict compliance with data privacy and payment security standards is absolutely critical. The Events Calendar Compliance guides are designed to help you navigate your responsibilities and understand exactly how our plugins assist in keeping your users’ data both safe and legal.

Whether you need to ensure your checkout process meets rigorous payment security standards or you want to verify that your attendee data collection adheres to global privacy laws, these resources provide the clarity you need to operate with confidence.
