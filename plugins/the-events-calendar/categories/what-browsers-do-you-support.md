## Browser Support

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/what-browsers-do-you-support/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/what-browsers-do-you-support/)

All our plugins should work with [the latest versions](https://updatemybrowser.org/) of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Opera, along with Microsoft’s Edge.

Our plugins use HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.

If you experience a problem with one of our plugins on the latest version of a current browser, please post to our [help desk](https://support.theeventscalendar.com/submit_ticket) and include a screenshot of your [SupportDetails.com](http://supportdetails.com/) report from the browser you are reporting a conflict with.
