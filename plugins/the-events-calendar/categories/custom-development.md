## Can I Hire Your Team to Customize My Calendar?

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/custom-development/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/custom-development/)

Our support team doesn’t take on custom development, but we can point you to someone who does. For advanced customizations and third-party integrations, we recommend hiring an experienced developer through [Codeable](https://codeable.io/partners/the-events-calendar/?ref=gMH0m), one of our partners. Codeable connects you with vetted WordPress developers, including experts in The Events Calendar.

## What Does Our Support Cover?

Our support team helps you install, update and use the features our plugins offer. Writing or changing code, restyling calendar views, adding new functionality, and integrating with third-party plugins, themes or services are outside what support covers. See [What Kind of Support Do You Provide for Paid Plugins?](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/what-support-is-provided-for-license-holders/) for details.

## How Do I Hire a Developer Through Codeable?

1. Go to Codeable’s [The Events Calendar page](https://codeable.io/partners/the-events-calendar/?ref=gMH0m) and click **Get a free estimate**.
2. Describe your project. A clear, concise explanation of what you want to achieve, with links to the pages involved, helps Codeable match you with the right developer.
3. Review the estimate and choose whether to go ahead. You work with the developer directly through Codeable.

## How Much Does It Cost?

It depends on the project. Codeable bases its estimate on the scope of the work, how complex it is and how urgent it is. For current rates and fees, see [Codeable’s pricing page](https://www.codeable.io/pricing/).
