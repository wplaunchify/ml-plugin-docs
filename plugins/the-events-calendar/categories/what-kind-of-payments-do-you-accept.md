## What Kind of Payments Do You Accept?

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/what-kind-of-payments-do-you-accept/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/what-kind-of-payments-do-you-accept/)

We accept all major credit cards. That includes Visa, American Express, Mastercard, Discover, and JCB.

You can pay with PayPal as well. During the purchase process, you will be prompted to log in to your PayPal account to confirm the purchase.

Unfortunately, we do not accept orders over the phone or mail.
