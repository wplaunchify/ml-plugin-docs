## Configuring Eventbrite Tickets

**Source:** [https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/eventbrite-tickets-settings/](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/eventbrite-tickets-settings/)

The Eventbrite Tickets plugin allows us to quickly create events on your WordPress site and associate them with tickets over on Eventbrite.com.

Before you can use it and start adding tickets, however, you will need to get your website connected to your Eventbrite account. Connecting is quick and easy:

1. Navigate to your **WP Admin → Events → Settings → Integrations → Import** and click the *Connect to Eventbrite* button.
2. You will be redirected to your [Eventbrite.com](https://www.eventbrite.com/) account.
3. After logging in, you will be asked to allow our *ea.theeventscalendar.com*application to access your account.
4. Once you click “Allow,” your site will be connected and you’ll be redirected to your imports page where you can [import your first event from Eventbrite](https://theeventscalendar.com/knowledgebase/k/importing-events-from-eventbrite-com/).

And that’s it! From there, you’re ready to begin creating events. If you’re unsure how to get started, check out our [Eventbrite Tickets new user primer](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/eventbrite-tickets/) as further reading 🙂

## Eventbrite Import Settings

You can import Eventbrite events with the Event Aggregator tool (WP Admin → Events → Import).

To import events and display tickets on your site, select the Eventbrite Import Origin.

![Eventbrite Import Origin](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/eventbriteonetime.jpg)

Events imported with Event Aggregator’s “Other URL” will not display tickets.

You can change the “Default Status,” “Default Event Category,” and “Show Map” options for Eventbrite imports under “Eventbrite Import Settings” by navigating to **WP Admin → Events → Settings → Imports** tab.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Eventbrite-Import-Settings.jpg)

Do also note that your Eventbrite imports will obey the [Event Update Authority settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/software/the-events-calendar/event-import-settings/#update-authority).

## Remove authorization

If you need to disconnect your site from Eventbrite, go to **Events → Settings → Integrations → Import** and click the **Disconnect** button next to “Refresh your connection to Eventbrite”.

![Eventbrite settings](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Eventbrite-Tickets-Settings-Overview-screenshot.jpg)
