## Adding Privacy Policy link to Kadence Form

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/adding-privacy-policy-link-to-kadence-form/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/adding-privacy-policy-link-to-kadence-form/)

Within the Kadence Form Block options, you can add a privacy policy link to the “accept” field in a couple of different ways.

First add a new field to your form using the “add new field button”.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/screen_shot_add_field.jpg)

2. Next add select the field type to be “accept”

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/screen_shot_set_field_type.jpg)

3. Scroll down to the field settings, there you will see an option to “Show Policy Link.” This will add a link above your accept field. You can define the text of this link and define the link URL.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/screenshot_above_link.jpg)

This will appear like the below image:

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/screen_shot_above_link_example.jpg)

4. Another option is to use the privacy policy placeholder in the fields label: {privacy_policy}

This will appear like the below image pulling in the title of your privacy policy page and the url of your privacy policy page.

![](https://docs.nexcess.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/screen_shot_inline_privacy_link.jpg)
