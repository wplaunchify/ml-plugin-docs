# Store Credit

			Store Credit for WooCommerce gives customers credit they can spend in your store. Each credit is a coupon with a balance, tied to the customer’s email, that they can spend over several orders until it runs out or expires. Send credit from your admin, or sell credit and gift cards as products.

## Requirements

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

- WordPress 6.0 or later, WooCommerce 8.2 or later, and PHP 7.4 or later.
- WooCommerce’s **Enable coupons** setting turned on.
- Compatible with WooCommerce’s cart and checkout blocks and High-Performance Order Storage.
- Works alongside Avalara AvaTax, PDF Invoices & Packing Slips and WooCommerce Shipping & Tax.

## Installation

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

Download Store Credit from your WooCommerce.com account, or install it from **WooCommerce » Extensions** in your store’s admin. Updates come through your WooCommerce.com connection. Then follow [Get started with Store Credit](https://kestrelwp.com/docs/get-started-with-store-credit/) to choose your settings and send your first credit.

## Features

[↑ Back to top](#doc-title)

- Send credit to customers from **WooCommerce » Send store credit**, with a note, an expiry date and product restrictions, and email it to them ([Get started with Store Credit](https://kestrelwp.com/docs/get-started-with-store-credit/#send-store-credit)).
- A coupon list on the cart and checkout, a **Store credit** page in My Account with the customer’s total balance, and one-click redeem links ([what customers see](https://kestrelwp.com/docs/get-started-with-store-credit/#what-customers-see)).
- Credit with or without tax included, and optionally applied to shipping ([settings](https://kestrelwp.com/docs/get-started-with-store-credit/#settings)).
- A **Store credit** product type for selling credit and gift cards, with preset or custom amounts ([Sell credit and gift cards with Store Credit](https://kestrelwp.com/docs/store-credit-sell-credit-and-gift-cards/)).
- Usage tracking per coupon, and credit restored automatically when orders are cancelled or refunded ([Manage Store Credit coupons](https://kestrelwp.com/docs/manage-store-credit-coupons/)).
