## Cancel your Subscription (Auto-renewal)

**Source:** [https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-cancel-your-subscription-auto-renewal/](https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-cancel-your-subscription-auto-renewal/)

In this article, we will provide an easy guide on how to cancel your auto-renewal subscription for ClickWhale pro.

If you purchased a ClickWhale annual license, an auto-renewal is set by default. You will receive an email reminder a week before your upcoming payment.

If you’d prefer to cancel your subscription, start by logging into your [Freemius account](https://users.freemius.com/).

After you log in, click the **Renewals & Billing** in the sidebar menu. Then select your ClickWhale subscription, and your subscription information will appear on the right side of your screen, click the **Cancel Auto-Renew** link.

![How To Cancel Auto Renewal](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Cancel-Auto-Renewal.png)

Remember that once your license expires, you will lose the following benefits:

**Price Lock:** The price at which you initially purchase ClickWhale Pro will remain the same, even if prices increase.
**Automatic Renewal**: You won’t have to worry about forgetting to renew your license.
**Continuous Updates**: You will always have access to the latest updates.

FAQs

**Can I cancel my subscription at any time?**

Yes. You can cancel your subscription’s auto-renewal at any time through your Freemius account. Your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

**Will canceling auto-renewal immediately deactivate my ClickWhale Pro license?**

No. Canceling auto-renewal only prevents your subscription from renewing. You will continue to have access to ClickWhale Pro until your current license expires.

**Where do I manage my subscription?**

You can manage your subscription by logging into your Freemius account, opening **Renewals & Billing**, selecting your ClickWhale subscription, and clicking **Cancel Auto-Renew**.

**What benefits will I lose after my license expires?**

Once your license expires, you will no longer receive plugin updates, retain your original renewal price (price lock), or have automatic renewals enabled.

**Will I receive a reminder before my subscription renews?**

Yes. If your subscription is set to auto-renew, ClickWhale sends a reminder email approximately one week before your next renewal payment.

## Delete My Account

**Source:** [https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-delete-my-account/](https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-delete-my-account/)

If you no longer wish to use your ClickWhale Pro account and want to delete it, you can easily do so via the [Freemius dashboard](https://users.freemius.com/).

Go to the [Freemius login page](https://users.freemius.com/) and log in to your account. Once logged in, navigate to the sidebar menu and click on **My Profile**. Scroll to the bottom of the My Profile page and click on the **Close Account** button.

![Docs Freemius Delete Account](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/docs-freemius-delete-account.png)

## Important Reminder

Before proceeding with account deletion, make sure to cancel all active subscriptions. This step is essential to prevent any unexpected charges or disruptions.

## Renew your License

**Source:** [https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-renew-your-license/](https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-renew-your-license/)

When you purchase a **ClickWhale Pro license**, you’ll receive a **license key valid for one year** from the date of purchase. ClickWhale Pro operates on an **annual subscription model**, similar to many popular premium WordPress plugins. Even if your license expires, **your data remains fully accessible**, ensuring uninterrupted access to your existing links, stats, and settings

You can **renew your ClickWhale Pro license** at any time after it expires to regain access to updates and priority support.

## How to renew my license?

If your **ClickWhale Pro license key** is nearing its expiration date, you’ll receive an **email reminder** with a direct link and step-by-step **instructions on how to renew your license**.

If you don’t receive the renewal email or accidentally delete it, you can still **renew your ClickWhale Pro license** by logging into your [Freemius account](https://clickwhale.pro/account/).

![Freemius login page](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/freemius-login-page.png)

Once logged in, click on **“Licenses”** in the sidebar menu. Locate the subscription you want to renew, then click the **“Renew”** button in the subscription details on the right side of the screen.

![License tab](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/license-tab-1024x464.png)

Complete the renewal process by entering your billing information, review your order, and complete the checkout process.

![Checkout tab](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/checkout-tab-1024x518.png)

## What happens if my license expires?

Once your **ClickWhale Pro license** expires, the plugin will continue to function with its existing features. However, you will **no longer receive plugin updates** or have access to **priority email support** until the license is renewed.

For example, if you’re using the **latest version of ClickWhale Pro** and your premium subscription expires today, you’ll still retain access to all current features. However, if a new version is released next month with enhancements or features you’d like to use, you’ll need to **renew your license** to update the plugin. Renewing your license grants you access to **all future updates**, **new features**, and continued **priority email support** for the next year.

## What are the auto-renewal benefits?

#### Benefits of Enabling Automatic License Renewal for ClickWhale Pro

By choosing to **automatically renew your ClickWhale Pro license**, you gain the following benefits:

- **Price Lock Guarantee**: Secure the original price of your ClickWhale Pro plan—even if future prices increase.
- **Hassle-Free Renewal**: No need to worry about missing renewal deadlines—your license renews automatically each year.
- **Continuous Access to Updates**: Stay up to date with the **latest ClickWhale Pro features, improvements, and security updates** without interruption.

## Does ClickWhale offer a lifetime license?

Yes, ClickWhale offers a lifetime license. To purchase or upgrade your license to a lifetime license, follow our guide on [how-to upgrade your license here](https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-upgrade-your-license/).

A lifetime license works exactly the way it sounds; you only need to pay once and get ClickWhale Pro and all future updates for life. There are no subscription or hidden costs.

## FAQs

**Can I renew my license after it has expired?**

Yes. You can renew your ClickWhale Pro license at any time after it expires to regain access to plugin updates and priority email support.

**What happens if my license expires?**

Your existing ClickWhale Pro installation will continue to work with its current features. However, you will no longer receive plugin updates or priority email support until you renew your license.

**How do I renew my ClickWhale Pro license?**

If your license is nearing expiration, you’ll receive a renewal email with instructions. If you don’t receive the email, you can log in to your Freemius account, open the **Licenses** section, click **Renew**, and complete the checkout process.

**What are the benefits of enabling automatic renewal?**

Automatic renewal helps you keep your original subscription price, ensures uninterrupted access to plugin updates, and renews your license each year without requiring manual action.

**Does ClickWhale offer a lifetime license?**

Yes. ClickWhale offers lifetime licenses. With a lifetime license, you pay once and receive ClickWhale Pro and all future updates without annual renewals.

## Upgrade your License

**Source:** [https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-upgrade-your-license/](https://clickwhale.pro/docs/article/how-to-upgrade-your-license/)

Upgrading your license to include more sites or switch from an annual subscription to a lifetime license is easy. The good thing is, no settings and customization made previously will be lost.

Upgrade options are available in your account area or on the pricing page. Here’s how:

## Option 1: Upgrade Through Your Account

Start by visiting the [Account page](https://clickwhale.pro/account/) and clicking the “Click here to Login Now” button. A new page will open in a new tab with the Freemius login form. Log in your email address and password to sign in to your dashboard.

![How To Upgrade Your License 1st Set Up](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Upgrade-your-License-Set-Up-1.png)

Within your customer account, navigate to **‘Licenses’** and select the row corresponding to the license you wish to upgrade.

![How To Upgrade Your License 2nd Set Up](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Upgrade-your-License-Image-2-1024x275.png)

A form with the information of your current plan will appear on the right side. Click on the **‘Upgrade** **to’** dropdown menu and select either 3 sites or 10 sites, depending on the number of sites you will be using the license for. This will open the checkout modal form.

![How To Upgrade Your License 3rd Set Up](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Upgrade-your-License-Image-3-1024x259.png)

Within the checkout modal, select either an annual or lifetime license, and the appropriate discount will be applied automatically.

![How To Upgrade Your License Set up 4](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Upgrade-your-License-Image-4-1024x713.png)

Lastly, enter your billing information, review your order, and complete the checkout process.

## Option 2: Upgrade Through Pricing Page

Start by visiting the [Pricing page](https://clickwhale.pro/pricing/). Toggle between **yearly** and **lifetime** options to choose the type of license you want. Click the ‘**Get Started**‘ button under your desired tier to open the checkout modal.

![How To Upgrade Your License Set up 5](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Upgrade-your-License-Image-5-1024x707.png)

In the checkout modal, click the link labeled ‘**Enter license key**‘.

![How To Upgrade Your License Set up 6](https://clickwhale.pro/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/How-to-Upgrade-your-License-Image-6-1024x371.png)

A text field will appear. Enter your license key and click **‘Apply’**. The appropriate discount will be applied automatically.

Finally, enter your billing information, review your order, and complete the checkout process.

## FAQs

**Can I upgrade my ClickWhale license at any time?**

Yes. You can upgrade your ClickWhale license whenever you need access to additional features or a higher plan.

**Will my existing ClickWhale data be preserved when I upgrade?**

Yes. Upgrading to ClickWhale Pro does not remove your existing links or settings. Once your Pro license is activated, you’ll have access to the additional Pro features.

**How do I upgrade my ClickWhale license?**

Go to **ClickWhale > License** in your WordPress dashboard and select **Upgrade**. You’ll be taken to the checkout page to complete the upgrade.

**Do I need to reinstall ClickWhale after upgrading?**

No. Your existing ClickWhale installation remains the same. Once the upgrade is complete, your new license and features will be available after your license is updated.
