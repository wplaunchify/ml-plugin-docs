## Remove terms

**Source:** [https://taxopress.com/docs/remove-terms-tab/](https://taxopress.com/docs/remove-terms-tab/)

TaxoPress has a feature that allows you to quickly remove terms from your content.

You will find this feature on the “[Manage Terms](https://taxopress.com/docs/introduction-to-manage-terms/)” screen in TaxoPress. This feature works for any WordPress taxonomy.

---

## Add terms to all posts

This feature allows you to remove a term to every post on your site. In the image below, I’m removing the term “security” from every post.

security remove term

- Click “Remove”.
- You’ll see a message confirming that the term was added to all posts:

---

## Remove terms to posts with specific terms

The “Remove terms” feature also allows you to add a term to only a post that already has a specific term. For example, you could automatically remove “Vegetables” as a term from any post that already has “Carrots”, “Spinach” or “Cabbages” as a term.

- In the image below, I’m removing the term “Food” from any post which already has the terms “Carrots”.
- Click “Add”.

## Merge Terms

**Source:** [https://taxopress.com/docs/merge-terms/](https://taxopress.com/docs/merge-terms/)

TaxoPress has a feature that allows you to merge terms together. You will find this feature on the “[Manage Terms](https://taxopress.com/docs/introduction-to-manage-terms/)” screen in TaxoPress.

TaxoPress can automatically merge Terms that exist on your site. This is very useful for cleaning up Terms that were created by mistake.

In the image below, I have three Terms that are typos: “Blloggingg”, “Bloggingg”, and “Bloging”. I want to merge them all into the “Blogging” term.

The Terms you want to merge must already exist and be added to content on your site.

merge terms

Click the “Rename” button and you’ll see a message like the screenshot below.

If you’re unsure what terms need merging, try the “Suggest Terms to Merge” button. This will automatically search for and find similar terms.

## Delete Unused Terms

**Source:** [https://taxopress.com/docs/remove-rarely-used-terms/](https://taxopress.com/docs/remove-rarely-used-terms/)

TaxoPress has a feature that allows you to quickly remove Terms that are rarely used on your site. This feature works for any WordPress taxonomy.

You will find this feature on the “[Manage Terms](https://taxopress.com/docs/introduction-to-manage-terms/)” screen in TaxoPress.

- Choose the minimum number of uses for Terms that you will keep. In the example below, any Term that has been used 0, 1, 2, 3 or 4 times will be deleted.
- Click the “Check Terms” button to see how many terms will be deleted.
- Click the “Delete Terms” button to remove the terms.
