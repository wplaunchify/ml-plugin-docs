## Google reCaptcha

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/google-recaptcha-form-adv-block/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/google-recaptcha-form-adv-block/)

**NOTE:** To view a list of all available add-ons and enable the ones you want to use, navigate to **MemberDash** > **Modules**

Enabling Google reCaptcha adds a **reCaptcha**option under the MemberDash****Modules options to improve the security of your MemberDash registration and/or login forms.

![](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/MemberDash-reCaptcha-enable.png)Enable the MemberDash reCaptcha moduleYou can improve the security of your MemberDash registration and/or login forms by enabling the Google reCaptcha option under the MemberDash****Settings

Once enabled, you can paste your Google reCaptcha Site key and Secret key. You can also choose which MemberDash forms to enable reCaptcha on.

**NOTE:** Currently, the module only supports Google reCaptcha v3.

![](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/MemDash-reCaptcha-Keys.png)Add reCaptcha Keys here![](https://www.liquidweb.com/support/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/image4-1-400x300.png)[Click here to register your site and get your Google reCaptcha API keys.](https://www.google.com/recaptcha/admin)
