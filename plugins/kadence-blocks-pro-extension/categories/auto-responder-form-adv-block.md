## Kadence Forms and Auto Respond Emails

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/auto-responder-form-adv-block/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-blocks/auto-responder-form-adv-block/)

The [Kadence Form](https://www.liquidweb.com/docs/kadence-blocks/form-block/) and [Form (Adv) Blocks](https://www.liquidweb.com/docs/kadence-blocks/advanced-form-block/) both support adding an **Auto Respond Email**. This feature is only available with [Kadence Blocks Pro](https://www.liquidweb.com/software/kadence/).

To use the *Kadence Forms Auto Respond Emails* you must have the **Kadence Blocks Pro**plugin installed and activated on the website. [You can click here to learn how to get started with Kadence Blocks Pro](https://www.liquidweb.com/docs/kadence-blocks/kadence-blocks-pro-plugin/).

## What is an Auto Respond Email

When enabled, an automatic email is sent whenever a form entry is submitted.  This could be useful for many reasons such as letting a visitor know you’ve receive their message, or perhaps informing them you’re away on vacation, or even alerting a third-party that a message has been submitted.

Both **Kadence Forms** and **Kadence Forms (Adv)** support Auto Respond Emails. Once your form has been created and you’ve enabled the Auto Respond Emails action, the Auto Respond Email Settings configuration is identical.

## Creating a Kadence Form with an Auto Respond Email

### Creating a Kadence Form

Get started by adding a **Form Block** to a page.

Once your form has been created, you’ll need to enable the Auto Respond Email action.

- In the form settings, click on the **Actions After Submit** section.
- Scroll down and check the **Auto Respond Email** option.![Kadence Form - Actions After Submit](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Screenshot-2025-05-15-at-12.22.32 PM-469x1024.png)### Creating a Kadence Form (Adv)

Get started by adding a **Form (Adv) Block** to a page.

Once your form has been created, you’ll need to enable the Auto Respond Email action.

- In the Form (Adv) Block Settings -> General Tab, click on the **Submit Actions** section.
- From the dropdown, select the **Auto Respond Email** setting.![Kadence Form Adv - Submit Actions](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Screenshot-2025-05-15-at-12.23.48 PM.png)
## Configure Your Auto Respond Email

After enabling Auto Respond Email for either the **Kadence Form** or **Kadence Form (Adv)**, you will now see a new **Auto Respond Email Settings** section.  This section is identical for both form blocks.

**Email Subject:** The subject of your auto-response email.

**Email Message:** The content of your auto-response email.

**From Email:** The from email address.

**From Name:** The from name.

**Reply To:** If you prefer replies, go to a different email address, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this blank.

**Cc:** Enter an email address to send a copy of the email to (optional).

**Bcc:** Enter an email address to send a blind copy to (optional).

**Send as HTML email?** When enabled, the email will be sent in HTML format. Disable this option to send a plain text message.

**Override Email to Address:** optionally add an email address to send the auto response to.  This could be useful to send an alert to a third-party without sending the actual form details.  Leave this blank to default to the sender’s email address.

When you’re done, be sure to save your changes.![Kadence Form - Auto Respond Email Settings](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Screenshot-2025-05-15-at-12.21.56 PM-355x1024.png)
## Testing Your Auto Respond Email

To test your auto-responding email, submit an entry for your form using an accessible email address. Then check your inbox to find the auto-response email.
