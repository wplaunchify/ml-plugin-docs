## Target WooCommerce Cart

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-conversions/target-woocommerce-cart/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-conversions/target-woocommerce-cart/)

If you are using WooCommerce with Kadence Conversions, there are some specific settings that will allow you to personalize the conversion item for specific shoppers and their interactions with your storefront. Change settings based on cart totals or if they’re purchasing a specific product or products.

![Kadence Conversions - Target WooCommerce Cart](https://res.cloudinary.com/lwcom/images/w_556,h_476,c_scale/f_auto,q_auto/v1779388151/prod/Screenshot-2026-05-21-at-1.28.31-PM/Screenshot-2026-05-21-at-1.28.31-PM.png?_i=AA)
## Only Show Cart Currency Total is More Than

Toggle this setting on and then set the minimum cart total value.

## Only Show When Cart Currency Total is Less Than

Toggle this setting on and then set the maximum cart total value.

## Only Show If Specific Products in Cart

Toggle this setting on and then select the products you’d like the conversion shown for. You can toggle as many products as needed.

## Hide If Specific Products in Cart

Toggle this setting on and then select the products you’d like the conversion hidden for. You can toggle as many products as needed.
