## Getting started: Creating your First Kadence Conversion

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-conversions/getting-started-creating-your-first-kadence-conversion/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-conversions/getting-started-creating-your-first-kadence-conversion/)

Kadence Conversions will be a left sidebar option below Settings with the Kadence logo next to it.

![Kadence Conversions navigation.](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/kadence-conversions-started.png)On the “All Conversion Items” page, click “Add New” at the top of the page.

![Add new conversion](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/add-conversion.png)Next, choose which type of Kadence Conversion element you’d like to work with. You can choose popup, slide-in, or banner.

![choose your conversion type](https://www.liquidweb.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/kadence-conversion-choose-type-1024x638.png)*Choose which type of conversion you’d like to build. You can build unlimited conversions with Kadence Conversions.*You’ll then choose either a blank conversion or use one of the starter popups, slide-in, or banners to help you get started. You can customize the images, the colors, and all other settings later.
