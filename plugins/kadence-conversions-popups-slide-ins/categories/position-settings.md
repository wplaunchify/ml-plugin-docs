## Position Settings

**Source:** [https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-conversions/position-settings/](https://www.kadencewp.com/help-center/docs/kadence-conversions/position-settings/)

Position settings will determine where on the screen your conversion item will appear.

**Popups** can be positioned horizontally (center, left, or right) and vertically (top, middle, bottom) on the screen.

**Slide-ins** can be positioned horizontally (center, left, or right) and vertically (top, middle, bottom) on the screen.

For **banners**, you can position either top, middle or bottom of the screen.
