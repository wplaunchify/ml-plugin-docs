## PDF Invoice & Packing

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/pdf-invoice-packing/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/pdf-invoice-packing/)

In order to hide bundles, bundled products, or both in PDF invoice & order details, you can check the *Compatible* tab of WPC Product Bundles plugin settings to find the plugin you’re using for packaging.

We have integrated this feature for two following plugins:

- WooCommerce PDF Invoices & Packing Slips
- WooCommerce PDF Invoices, Packing Slips, Delivery Notes & Shipping Labels
