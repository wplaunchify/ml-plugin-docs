## Tax Rate

Tax will be calculated based on your store settings at **WooCommerce >> Settings >> Tax**.

In this section, users can configure the *Tax classes, Shipping tax class, Additional tax classes, Tax in price display in shop, cart & checkout,* and *Tax total display*.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-tax.png)

In the *General* tab of the**Product Data** section of the bundle page, you can choose *a tax status* and *a tax class* for your bundle there:

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-tax-class-e1594133285863.png)
