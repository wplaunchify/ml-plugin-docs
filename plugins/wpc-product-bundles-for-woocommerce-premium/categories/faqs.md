## 1. Composite + Grouped in a bundle?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/1-composite-grouped-in-a-bundle/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/1-composite-grouped-in-a-bundle/)

#### Question 1: Can I add a grouped product or a composite product to another bundle to use as a bundled product?

**Answer:** No. All products created by [WPC Product Bundles](https://wpclever.net/downloads/woocommerce-product-bundle), [WPC Composite Products](https://wpclever.net/downloads/wpc-composite-products-for-woocommerce), or [WPC Grouped Products](https://wpclever.net/downloads/wpc-grouped-product-for-woocommerce) plugin are of equivalent product types.

You can observe that fact when choosing a product type from the Product Data drop-down list. Therefore, smart bundles, composite products, and smart grouped products CANNOT be added inside one another. Even if you can search for and add them in the back-end, but in the front-end, they won’t be visible or functional as normal.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-1.png)

In order to add extra items to a Smart Bundle, Composite Product, or Smart Grouped, you can use [WPC Frequently Bought Together](https://wpclever.net/downloads/woocommerce-bought-together) or [WPC Force Sells](https://wpclever.net/downloads/wpc-force-sells) plugin. After installing these plugins, users can add extra products in the Bought Together tab or Force Sells tab.

## 10. Hide bundle’s name before bundled products?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/10-hide-bundles-name-before-bundled-products/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/10-hide-bundles-name-before-bundled-products/)

#### Question 10: How to hide the bundle’s name before bundled products in cart and checkout?

**Answer:** Please go to**WPClever >> Product Bundles >>** in “Hide bundle name before bundled products” choose “Yes” **>> Update options**. In cart and checkout, the title of each bundled product will change from “Product Bundle 1 → Product 1” to just “Product 1”.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-10.png)

*Note:*Doing this won’t hide the main product (eg. Product Bundle 1), this is to just hide the bundle’s name in the title of each bundled item on the cart and checkout pages.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-wpcstore-hide-bundled-products-cart.gif)

## 12. Unlink individual products in bundles?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/12-unlink-individual-products-in-bundles/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/12-unlink-individual-products-in-bundles/)

#### Question 12: I just want to sell some products in bundles, but not as individual products in my shop. How do I prevent customers from finding them and adding them to the cart?

**Answer:**

> It’s required that all bundled products are set as Public, not Private or Hidden, so that our plugin can find and add them to the smart bundle.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-visibility.png)

There are two things you can do to prevent buyers from reaching those bundled items:

1/ Disable “Link to individual product” by choosing “No” for this option in **WPClever >> Product Bundles**.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-12-1.png)

2/ Hide them from your shop archive and search by adjusting their visibility to *Hidden*.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-12-2.png)

Then buyers won’t be able to click through the title of bundled products to visit individual product pages anymore.

## 14. Display discounted amount on bundles?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/14-display-discounted-amount-on-bundles/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/14-display-discounted-amount-on-bundles/)

#### Question 14: How can I display the discounted amount on bundles?

**Answer:** Only bundles whose prices are automatically calculated can display the “Saved text”. Please make sure the “Disable the auto-calculated price” is unchecked and fill in a discount in the corresponding box (**% or $**).

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-step-8.png)

Depending on the type of discount applied to your bundle, the saved text will display accordingly. The amount discount will override the percentage discount when both boxes are filled.

In order to show the Saved text in both Starting price & Subtotal, please enable “Change price” from the plugin settings. Then the saved text will be visible on all auto-priced bundles in both positions.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-pricing-price-display-3.png)

See more in the [Saved Text](https://doc.wpclever.net/docs/wpc-product-bundles-for-woocommerce/pricing-discounts/saved-text/) under the Pricing and Discounts section.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-wpcstore-custom-saved-text.png)

## 15. Set different min/max quantity limits?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/15-set-different-min-max-quantity-limits/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/15-set-different-min-max-quantity-limits/)

#### Question 15: Can I set different minimum & maximum quantities for bundled items in a bundle?

**Answer:** The answer is yes and no. In order to set up Min/Max quantity for bundles, you must first enable Custom quantity by choosing the checkbox “Buyer can change the quantity of bundled products”.

After that, you can set different minimum quantities by configuring a different predefined quantity for each item, then putting a check in the “Use the default quantity as Min?” option.

You CAN’T set up different maximum quantities for each item. The Min & Max quantity limit for the whole bundle is the min and max of total units for all bundled items.

If you enabled the “Use the default quantity as Min?” and also fill in any of the 2 Min boxes with a number, the default quantity will be prioritized as min quantity limit for each item.

The logic when it comes to setting the Min-Max should also be considered upon bundle creation. The multiplication of each item’s min limit and the number of bundled products should be equal or lower than the max limit for all items.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-15.png)

## 16. How to disable the universal quantity box next to the Add to Cart button

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/16-how-to-disable-the-universal-quantity-box-next-to-the-add-to-cart-button/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/16-how-to-disable-the-universal-quantity-box-next-to-the-add-to-cart-button/)

#### Question 16: I would like to disable the universal quantity box next to the Add to Cart button so that each customer can buy only 1 bundle. How to do that?

**Answer:** In the bundle page, you can go to the *Inventory* tab then put a check to enable the “Sold Individually” option for that bundle.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/new-inventory-tab.png)

When this option is enabled, the universal quantity box next to the Add to Cart button will be hidden. Therefore, one buyer/customer can only add 1 unit of that bundle to an order. This option is to make sure each customer can only get 1 unit of the chosen bundle per single order.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/woosb-wpcstore-sold-individually.gif)

If you want to allow multiple bundles to be purchased in a single order per customer, remember to uncheck/disable this option from the *Inventory* tab of the bundle page.

#### Question 2: Can I use the ddSlick interface with WPC Product Bundles?

**Answer:** No. There’s no ddSlick interface integrated into WPC Product Bundles.

## 8. Exempt bundles from coupons?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/8-exempt-bundles-from-coupons/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/8-exempt-bundles-from-coupons/)

#### Question 8: I’ve already set up discounts for bundles, so how do I exclude bundles from coupon applications?

**Answer:** By default, coupons are still applicable to product bundles (thus, no restrictions on coupons). You can go to **WPClever >> Product Bundles >>** in the “Coupon Restrictions” option, choose a suitable one that suits your need:

- *Exclude bundles:* Auto-priced bundles will still accept coupons. Individual products are applicable to coupons but not when they are purchased altogether as a bundle.
- *Exclude bundled products*: Manually priced bundles will still accept coupons. Individual products are NOT applicable to coupons when they are purchased as individual items, not part of a bundle.

For both these options, the *fixed cart amount* coupon type is still applicable.

> If you want to completely exempt both bundles and bundled products from coupon applications, please choose the last option “Exclude both bundles and bundled products”.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-8-e1593094946139.png)

## 9. Out of Stock bundles?

**Source:** [https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/9-out-of-stock-bundles/](https://doc.wpclever.net/woosb/faqs/9-out-of-stock-bundles/)

#### Question 9: I create a bundle and all bundled products are in stock, but in the front-end, my bundle displays “Out of stock”. How to fix that?

**Answer:** First, please check if you have selected “Enable stock management at bundle level?”, then go to the *Inventory* tab to check the stock quantity configured.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-inventory-stock-bundle-level.png)

Normally after enabling this option and press **Update**, the stock quantity for the whole bundle in the Inventory tab will be **0**, you need to enter a number into this box to make it “**In stock**” and enable stock management.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-step-12-2.png)

You can also go to **WPClever >> Product Bundles >>** in the “Cart & Checkout” section, choose “Yes” for “Exclude un-purchasable products”, in the front-end, out-of-stock products will still be visible, but buyers cannot choose or add them to the cart.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-faq-9.png)

When the *Exclude unpurchasable* option is enabled, any out-of-stock products will be removed from the bundles in the front end:

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-wpcstore-exclude-unpurchasable-back-end.png)

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb-wpcstore-saved-text.png)
