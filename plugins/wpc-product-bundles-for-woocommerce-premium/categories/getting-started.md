### WPC Product Bundles for WooCommerce

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/woosb_772x250_2021.png)

**WPC Product Bundles for WooCommerce** is a powerful plugin developed by the WPClever team. This plugin is designed for bundling several products together with great discount offers to increase sales, promote cross-sell products, and boost your income.

You can learn more about the functionalities of this plugin in this [description](https://wpclever.net/downloads/woocommerce-product-bundle).

WPC Product Bundles comes in 2 versions:

- The free version: unlimited item limit, allows simple and subscription products.
- The Premium version: allows simple products, subscription products, variable products, and variations to be added with entire powerful features and regular updates.
