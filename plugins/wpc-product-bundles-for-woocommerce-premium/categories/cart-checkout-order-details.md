## Cart & Checkout Display

It’s impossible to hide the main bundle on cart and checkout pages, since the main virtual bundle is needed for increasing/decreasing the quantity of the whole bundle. Removing the main bundle will remove the whole deal from cart.

Users can choose to hide the sub-products completely or list their name under the main bundle on cart & checkout pages. This list can be a single line or an unordered list with each item in 1 line.

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/woosb-hide-bundled-products.png)

The option to list sub-product names in several lines was newly added in a recent update. In the frontend, it will be like:

![](https://doc-14bd3.kxcdn.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/woosb-hide-subproducts-in-separate-lines.png)**Bundle on cart page**
